Making it work

Getting the most from a caregiver support group

  1. 01

    Try two groups

    Each group has its own feel. Visit two before deciding.

  2. 02

    Bring one question

    A specific question, about bathing, money, guilt or the next step, gets the most useful answers.

  3. 03

    Line up coverage

    Use a respite program, a family member or an aide so the hour is truly the caregiver's own.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Where can I find a caregiver support group in Naples?

Baker Senior Center Naples (6200 Autumn Oaks Lane) holds caregiver support groups on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 1:00 to 2:25 p.m., led by clinical social workers. The Alzheimer's Support Network (1421 Pine Ridge Road) runs general support groups on Tuesdays and Thursdays and an online Adult Children group.

Are there online caregiver support groups?

Yes. Several Alzheimer's Support Network groups are hybrid or online, and the Alzheimer's Association (800-272-3900) registers caregivers for virtual support groups.

Can my loved one be cared for while I attend?

Baker Senior Center Naples runs a Dementia Respite Program on weekdays from 10:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., and the Alzheimer's Support Network hosts its Brain Fitness Center during some general support group meetings. Call ahead to confirm.

Is there caregiver help in Lee County too?

Yes. Senior Friendship Centers of Lee County, based in Fort Myers (239-275-1881), offers caregiving support and the RELIEF night and weekend respite program (239-236-8680).
