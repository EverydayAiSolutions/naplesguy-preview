What Pre-K and VPK students learn in Florida

Florida Voluntary Prekindergarten is open to eligible 4- and 5-year-olds and uses the Florida Early Learning and Developmental Standards for children ages 4 to kindergarten. This page summarizes those official expectations and the state’s Prekindergarten Family Guide.

What Pre-K and VPK children work toward

VPK is built around play, talk, and everyday routines. The official standards cover language and literacy, mathematical thinking, scientific inquiry, social studies, and other developmental areas.

Language and early literacy

  • Show an interest in books and reading, including choosing books and pretending to read.
  • Build phonological awareness by playing with rhymes and beginning sounds.
  • Name most letters and recognize some letter sounds.
  • Retell or reenact a story after it is read aloud, and ask and answer questions about it.
  • Use scribbles, letter-like shapes, or letters to convey meaning, and write their own name.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Prekindergarten Family Guide. Source: Florida Early Learning and Developmental Standards, 4 Years Old to Kindergarten (2017).

Prekindergarten Family Guide (PDF) ELA parent guides

Mathematical thinking

  • Immediately recognize, without counting, up to five objects (subitizing).
  • Count and identify the number sequence from 1 to 31.
  • Use one-to-one correspondence when counting objects in a row.
  • Construct and count sets of objects, and use counting or matching to tell which is more, less, or equal (up to 10 and beyond).

Source: Florida Early Learning and Developmental Standards, 4 Years Old to Kindergarten, Mathematical Thinking domain.

Early Learning and Developmental Standards (PDF)

Scientific inquiry

  • Explore and ask questions about the world through hands-on discovery.
  • Use simple tools and technologies, such as scales or cameras, to support exploration.

Source: Florida Early Learning and Developmental Standards, 4 Years Old to Kindergarten, Scientific Inquiry domain.

Early Learning and Developmental Standards (PDF)

Social studies and getting along

  • The official standards include a Social Studies domain, along with social and emotional development such as understanding feelings and taking turns.
  • Children also practice talking, listening, and working with others in a classroom or child-care setting.

Source: Florida Early Learning and Developmental Standards, 4 Years Old to Kindergarten.

Who can attend Florida VPK

The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, which serves Collier, Lee, Hendry, and Glades counties, says VPK is open to eligible 4- and 5-year-olds at no cost, regardless of family income.

A child must be 4 years old by September 1 of the school year, and the family must live in Florida. Families apply on the official Florida Early Learning Family Portal, then take the certificate of eligibility to a chosen provider.

Source: Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, VPK for parents.

Everyday practice

How parents can help at home

These ideas come from the official Florida parent guides for this grade, or from closely related official family pages. Short, regular practice works better than long, stressful sessions.

Things to try

  • Make books, poetry, and songs part of daily time together, and read aloud every day.
  • Ask open-ended questions such as “why” or “how,” and ask for more than a yes-or-no answer.
  • Play with rhymes and beginning sounds in words.
  • Leave simple notes around the house and invite the child to write or draw a note back, even if the writing is still scribbles or letter-like shapes.
  • Have real conversations, listen carefully, and respond to the child’s thoughts and questions.

Collier County Public Library

The official library site is a good place to find books at a child’s interest level, storytimes, and digital resources. Youth library cards are available for children from birth through age 17, and a parent or guardian needs to be present with identification for younger children.

Source: Collier County Public Library, Get a Card.

Assessments

Testing this year

VPK progress monitoring

The Florida Assessment of Student Thinking (FAST) includes progress-monitoring assessments in ELA Reading and Mathematics beginning in VPK, given three times a year.

A child’s VPK provider or school can explain which assessment is used and how results are shared. This is information for teachers and families, not a high-stakes test for a 4-year-old.

Source: Florida Department of Education, FAST Assessments.

Questions

Questions about Pre-K / VPK

These answers come from the official pages linked here. Details can change, so families should confirm them on those sites.

Is VPK the same as childcare?

VPK is Florida’s Voluntary Prekindergarten education program for eligible 4- and 5-year-olds. It can be offered by a school or a private provider. Families still use regular childcare, if they need it, around the VPK hours. Source: Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, VPK for parents.

How do Naples families apply for VPK?

Families apply on the official Florida Early Learning Family Portal, then take the certificate of eligibility to a chosen VPK provider. The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida can help at 239-935-6100. Source: Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida.
