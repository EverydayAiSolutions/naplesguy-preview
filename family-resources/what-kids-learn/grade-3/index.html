Florida · Third grade

What third graders learn in Florida schools

Third grade is a big reading year in Florida. Students are expected to read more fluently, write in cursive, and show their reading skills on FAST ELA Reading. Official rules for promotion to grade 4 are explained on the Florida Department of Education’s Third Grade Guidance page.

By subject

What third graders work toward

Reading to learn becomes more important, and math moves into multiplication, division, and fractions.

English language arts

  • Decode multisyllabic words and words with suffixes such as -ful, -less, and -est, and fluently read grade-level texts.
  • Write all uppercase and lowercase letters in cursive.
  • Write narrative, opinion, and expository texts that follow standard English grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling, and use grade-appropriate vocabulary in speaking and writing.
  • Summarize a text, identify the central idea and relevant details, and explain how text features contribute to meaning.
  • Discuss how characters grow and change, figure out unknown words using context and other strategies, compare how two authors present the same topic, and conduct research from multiple sources.

Source: Florida Department of Education, B.E.S.T. Parent Guide for Grade 3 English Language Arts.

Mathematics

  • Work with place value up to the thousands, and add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers accurately, including with regrouping.
  • Build an understanding of multiplication and division and their relationship, connect area to arrays and equal groups, and develop formulas for area and perimeter.
  • Understand numerators and denominators, represent fractions with models and number lines, and explain whether fractions are equivalent.
  • Extend geometric reasoning to lines and attributes of quadrilaterals.

Source: Florida Department of Education, B.E.S.T. Parent Guide for Grade 3 Mathematics.

Science

  • Florida’s science standards at this grade include the nature of science along with life, physical, and Earth and space science topics.

Social studies

  • Grade 3 social studies includes historical inquiry, geography (places, regions, physical and human systems), economics, and civics and government.
  • The official benchmarks are listed by strand on CPALMS and in Florida’s State Academic Standards for Social Studies.

Source: Florida’s State Academic Standards – Social Studies, 2023, Grade 3.

Worth knowing this year

Key milestones

Promotion to grade 4 and reading support

Florida law sets rules for promotion from grade 3 to grade 4 based on a student’s reading skills. The Florida Department of Education’s Third Grade Guidance page explains the current statutes, good-cause exemptions, alternative assessments, and mid-year promotion options.

Schools also identify students in kindergarten through grade 3 who need extra help in reading and provide additional support. Families should talk with the teacher early if reading feels hard, and confirm the current promotion rules with the school and the official FLDOE page.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Third Grade Guidance. Source: Section 1008.25, Florida Statutes.

Everyday practice

How parents can help at home

These ideas come from the official Florida parent guides for this grade, or from closely related official family pages. Short, regular practice works better than long, stressful sessions.

Things to try

  • Listen to the child read grade-level texts and talk about the central idea, details, and how characters change.
  • Practice cursive in short, practical ways, such as writing a name or a short thank-you note.
  • Use objects or drawings to show multiplication as equal groups, and to show fractions as parts of a whole.
  • Keep daily reading going over weekends and school breaks.
  • Visit the library and look for both stories and informational books.

Collier County Public Library

The official library site is a good place to find books at a child’s interest level, storytimes, and digital resources. Youth library cards are available for children from birth through age 17, and a parent or guardian needs to be present with identification for younger children.

Source: Collier County Public Library, Get a Card.

Assessments

Testing this year

Statewide tests in third grade

Students in third grade take FAST ELA Reading and FAST Mathematics.

FAST Reading and Math are given three times a year. PM1 and PM2 are for information. PM3 is the end-of-year score used for school accountability. Official 2026–2027 windows include PM2 from November 30, 2026, through January 22, 2027, and PM3 from May 3–28, 2027, for grades 3–10 Reading and 3–8 Mathematics.

Source: Florida Department of Education, FAST Assessments and K-12 Student Assessment. Source: 2026–2027 Florida Statewide Assessment Program Schedule.

Questions about Third grade

These answers come from the official pages linked here. Details can change, so families should confirm them on those sites.

What happens if a third grader struggles on FAST ELA Reading?

Florida law requires schools to support students who are not yet reading at grade level, and it sets rules for promotion to grade 4, including good-cause exemptions described by the Florida Department of Education. Families should read the official Third Grade Guidance page and talk with the school about the child’s results and options. Source: Florida Department of Education, Third Grade Guidance. Source: Section 1008.25, Florida Statutes.
