Florida · Tenth grade

What tenth graders learn in Florida schools

Tenth grade includes a key graduation assessment: Grade 10 FAST ELA Reading, which students must pass (or earn a concordant score) for a standard diploma. Algebra 1 is the other required statewide assessment if it has not already been passed.

By subject

What tenth graders work toward

Many students take English 2, the next math course in their sequence, a science course such as Biology if they have not already, and a social studies course in their school’s sequence.

English language arts

  • Many students take English 2 or English 2 Honors, as listed by Collier County Public Schools, with other options such as Advanced Placement or AICE available on some campuses.
  • B.E.S.T. high school ELA standards continue work in reading complex literary and informational texts, citing evidence, writing, research, and speaking and listening. Grade 10 FAST ELA Reading is the statewide reading assessment used for graduation.

Source: Collier County Public Schools, Literacy – English Language Arts. Source: Florida Department of Education, FAST Assessments and Academic Advisement.

CCPS literacy / course list B.E.S.T. ELA standards (PDF) FAST assessments

Mathematics

  • Students continue toward four math credits, including Algebra 1 and Geometry. A tenth grader may be in Geometry, Algebra 2, or another course, depending on earlier placement.
  • Official B.E.S.T. parent guides are available for Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, and other high school math courses.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement and B.E.S.T. Mathematics Parent Resources.

Math parent guides CCPS mathematics

Science

  • Students continue toward three science credits, including Biology 1 and two equally rigorous courses, with laboratory components as required on the official advisement flyer.
  • Students enrolled in Biology 1 take the Biology 1 EOC, which counts as 30 percent of the final course grade.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement and K-12 Student Assessment.

Graduation advisement (PDF) K-12 student assessment

Social studies

  • Students continue toward World History, U.S. History, and U.S. Government credits required for a standard diploma. The exact tenth-grade social studies course depends on the school’s sequence.
  • Official high school American history standards begin with Civil War and Reconstruction and continue through later periods; world history, government, and economics standards are also published for grades 9–12.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement. Source: Florida’s State Academic Standards – Social Studies, 2023, Grades 9–12.

Worth knowing this year

Key milestones

Grade 10 FAST ELA Reading

Students must pass Grade 10 ELA or earn a concordant score to meet standard diploma assessment requirements. Official pages also describe retake opportunities and options for some students with disabilities or eligible English language learners.

Grade 10 students also take B.E.S.T. Writing. FAST Reading is given three times a year, with PM3 used for school accountability.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement. Source: FAST Assessments.

Everyday practice

How parents can help at home

These ideas come from the official Florida parent guides for this grade, or from closely related official family pages. Short, regular practice works better than long, stressful sessions.

Things to try

  • Talk with the student about the Grade 10 ELA assessment early in the year, without adding panic, and ask the teacher what kind of practice is most useful.
  • Keep a simple system for deadlines, especially if the student has more than one writing or research assignment at a time.
  • Encourage reading widely, including nonfiction, and discuss how an author supports a claim.
  • Use official FLDOE and school resources before unofficial test-prep sites.

Collier County Public Library

The official library site is a good place to find books at a child’s interest level, storytimes, and digital resources. Youth library cards are available for children from birth through age 17, and a parent or guardian needs to be present with identification for younger children.

Source: Collier County Public Library, Get a Card.

Assessments

Testing this year

Assessments in tenth grade

Tenth graders take FAST ELA Reading three times a year, and they take B.E.S.T. Writing. Passing Grade 10 ELA (or a concordant score) is a standard diploma assessment requirement.

Students also take end-of-course exams for any EOC courses they are enrolled in, such as Algebra 1, Geometry, Biology 1, or U.S. History. Those EOCs count as 30 percent of the final course grade. Algebra 1 (or a comparative score) is also a diploma assessment requirement.

Official 2026–2027 windows include FAST PM2 from November 30, 2026, through January 22, 2027; Writing from March 29–April 9, 2027; and FAST PM3 from May 3–28, 2027, for grades 3–10 ELA Reading. Schools set actual testing days inside those windows.

Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement and K-12 Student Assessment. Source: 2026–2027 Florida Statewide Assessment Program Schedule.

Questions

Questions about Tenth grade

These answers come from the official pages linked here. Details can change, so families should confirm them on those sites.

What happens if a student does not pass Grade 10 FAST ELA Reading on the first try?

Florida provides retake opportunities for Grade 10 ELA Reading, and the official advisement flyer also describes concordant scores and other options for some students. Families should confirm the current rules and dates with the school and the Florida Department of Education. Source: Florida Department of Education, Academic Advisement and K-12 Student Assessment.
