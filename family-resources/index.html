Health & Safety

Pediatric emergency care, urgent care, vaccines, hurricane prep, and water safety.

Health, urgent care, and vaccines

In a medical emergency, call 911. For poison questions, Florida’s Poison Control Centers answer 1-800-222-1222 at any hour. The official pages below list pediatric emergency care in Collier County, children’s urgent care in Naples, the regional children’s hospital in Fort Myers, and county immunization clinics.

NCH Pediatric Emergency Department

NCH describes this as the only 24-hour pediatric emergency department in Collier County, designed for children from birth to age 18.

It is at NCH North Naples Hospital, 11190 Health Park Blvd, Naples, and is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

(239) 624-9135

NCH Pediatric ERNorth Naples Hospital location

Lee Health Kids’ Urgent Care in Naples

Lee Health’s official page lists walk-in care for infants through young adults up to age 21 at 3361 Pine Ridge Rd, Suite 101, Naples, with no appointment needed.

Published hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. every day, with parking on site at the Golisano Children’s Health Center. Call 911 for a medical emergency.

(239) 254-5920

Kids’ Urgent Care in Naples

Golisano Children’s Hospital Emergency Department

Lee Health describes Golisano Children’s Hospital in Fort Myers as the only children’s hospital between Tampa and Miami, and this emergency department as the only one in Lee County built just for children.

The official address is 9981 S HealthPark Dr, Fort Myers. The department is listed as open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

(239) 343-6260

Children’s emergency departmentGolisano Children’s Hospital

Florida Poison Control

Florida’s Poison Control Centers answer poisoning questions and emergencies at 1-800-222-1222, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

The official network page says the call is confidential, and that specialists can also help in Spanish.

1-800-222-1222

Florida Poison Control Centers

Florida Department of Health in Collier County

The Florida Department of Health in Collier County is the official county health department. The published main number is 239-252-8200.

Its immunization clinics ask families to bring a photo ID and previous immunization records. Appointments are scheduled at 239-252-8207 in Naples.

(239) 252-8200

DOH-CollierImmunizations

Hurricane prep, evacuation zones, and swim lessons

Collier County Emergency Management is the official source for evacuation zones, the Special Needs Program, and other all-hazards preparation. County parks list aquatic programs and swim classes through official registration.

Evacuation zones and emergency alerts

Collier County Emergency Management’s official page includes tools to determine an evacuation zone and to sign up for the Special Needs Program.

The department is at 3299 Tamiami Trail East, Naples. The published number is 239-252-4311, which is also listed as Collier 311, with hours Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

(239) 252-4311

Program schedules change by season, so families should confirm current classes on the official registration pages.

Schools & Early Learning

Enrollment, school calendars, VPK, childcare, and special needs supports.

Schools, enrollment, and VPK

New students enroll in Collier County Public Schools through the district’s official online system. Florida’s Voluntary Prekindergarten program is administered locally by the Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida.

What kids learn in each grade

A guide on this site explains, in plain English, what Florida students work toward in Pre-K through 12th grade, with official parent guides, lessons, practice, videos, and testing information.

Open the grade-by-grade guide

Collier County Public Schools enrollment

Kindergarten through 12th grade students who are new to the district, and students returning after withdrawing, enroll through Collier County Public Schools Centralized Online Enrollment at collierschools.com/enroll.

The official enrollment page lists registration help at 239-377-0547, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., and an email address at registration@collierschools.com.

(239) 377-0547

Collier County Public Schools enrollmentStudent enrollment guide

Collier County Public Schools calendar

The official Collier County Public Schools calendar, including first days, holidays, and other district dates, is posted on the district’s calendars page.

The Family Events calendar on this site also uses those official no-school days so families can look for camps and activities on days school is closed.

Official Collier County Public Schools calendarsNo-school days

Florida Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK)

The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, which serves Collier, Lee, Hendry, and Glades counties, says VPK is a state-funded program open to eligible 4- and 5-year-olds, regardless of family income.

A child must be 4 years old by September 1 of the school year, and the family must live in Florida. Families apply on the official Florida Early Learning Family Portal, then take the certificate of eligibility to a chosen provider.

(239) 935-6100

VPK for parentsFlorida Early Learning Family Portal

Florida private school directory

The Florida Department of Education publishes an official private school directory through Florida School Choice, which families can search by county.

This directory is a state listing of private schools. It is not a recommendation of any school.

Florida private school directory

Childcare and early learning

Families looking for childcare can start with Florida’s official licensed-provider search and with the Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, which offers Child Care Resource & Referral and School Readiness help for eligible families.

Licensed childcare provider search

The Florida Department of Children and Families publishes a public search of licensed child care providers, which families can use to look up a center or home.

A license search is a starting point. Families should still confirm current hours, openings, and inspection information on the official record and with the provider.

Florida DCF childcare search

Child Care Resource & Referral

The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida offers Child Care Resource & Referral to families looking for child care centers, family child care homes, and school-age care in Collier, Lee, Hendry, and Glades counties.

Families can call 239-935-6100 or use the Coalition’s online request form. The Coalition announced that its Naples office closes November 30, 2026, with services continuing from the Fort Myers office at 2675 Winkler Ave, Suite 300.

(239) 935-6100

Child Care Resource & ReferralEarly Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida

School Readiness

The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida says Florida’s School Readiness program provides affordable early education and child care and is intended to support working families.

Eligibility and openings can change. The official School Readiness page and a Family Resource Advisor at 239-935-6100 are the places to confirm current details.

(239) 935-6100

School Readiness for parents

Special needs supports

Official starting points include Collier County Public Schools Exceptional Student Education, Florida’s Early Steps program for infants and toddlers, and FDLRS family support.

Exceptional Student Education (Collier County Public Schools)

Collier County Public Schools’ Exceptional Student Education department serves students with disabilities. The official page says the district has more than 6,000 students with disabilities, and that every school has an ESE program specialist.

The published ESE phone number is 239-377-0108, and the email is teachingandlearning@collierschools.com.

(239) 377-0108

Exceptional Student Education (Collier County Public Schools)

Florida Early Steps

Florida’s Early Steps program provides early intervention for eligible infants and toddlers, from birth to 36 months, with developmental delays, disabilities, or certain at-risk conditions.

A parent, caregiver, or physician can make a referral. The official program phone number is 1-800-218-0001.

1-800-218-0001

Early Steps eligibility and screeningFlorida Early Steps

Florida Diagnostic and Learning Resources System (Island Coast)

Florida Diagnostic and Learning Resources System (Island Coast), based in Fort Myers, is part of Florida’s official Florida Diagnostic and Learning Resources System and offers family and educator support for students with disabilities.

The official site also points families to the statewide FDLRS Family Help Desk. The published office number is 239-337-8363.

(239) 337-8363

Florida Diagnostic and Learning Resources System (Island Coast)

Libraries, Parks & Beaches

Storytimes, playgrounds, splash pads, beach parking, and rip current safety.

Libraries and storytimes

Collier County Public Library branches host toddler, preschool, and family storytimes, plus other children’s programs. Times vary by branch, so families should check the official events listing before they go.

Collier County Public Library

The official library site and events listing cover all county branches. The Headquarters Library is at 2385 Orange Blossom Dr, Naples, and the published phone number is 239-252-7350.

Some Headquarters storytimes use tickets given the morning of the program in the children’s room, on a first-come, first-served basis. The official event listing for each program has the current details.

(239) 252-7350

Library homepageStorytimes and events

Parks, splash pads, and playgrounds

Collier County Parks & Recreation is the official starting point for county parks, aquatic programs, and class registration. This site’s Places to Go page gathers parks, splash pads, and playgrounds families can visit on an ordinary day.

Collier County Parks & Recreation

The official Collier Parks site lists county parks, beaches, and aquatic parks, and it has online registration for programs, classes, and camps.

Hours, fees, and which programs are open change through the year, so families should confirm details on the official registration pages.

Collier County ParksAquatic parksProgram registration

Beaches, parking, and water safety

Beach parking rules differ for county parks, City of Naples beach ends, and Florida State Parks. Water quality and rip-current conditions also change, so families should check official information before they swim.

Beach parking and resident permits

Collier County’s official beach parking page says county residents can get a beach parking permit with proof of residency, and that visitors who are not eligible for the permit pay a flat $10 per day through Mobile Pay to Park.

City of Naples beach ends and nearby state parks have their own rules and fees. The Naples Beaches Guide on this site gathers those official parking, restroom, and access details in one place.

Water quality and rip currents

The National Weather Service explains how to recognize a rip current and what to do if someone is caught in one.

Help Lines & Family Support

211, WIC, food assistance, and the Florida Abuse Hotline.

Help lines, WIC, and food assistance

These official numbers and portals help families find local services, nutrition support, and food assistance. In an emergency, call 911. To report suspected abuse or neglect, call the Florida Abuse Hotline, or 911 if someone is in immediate danger.

211 in Collier County

United Way of Collier and the Keys operates 211, a non-emergency line that connects families to local services such as food, housing, health programs, and disaster relief.

Families can dial 211 or 239-263-4211, or text a ZIP code to 898211, to reach a specialist.

(239) 263-4211

211 United Way of Collier and the Keys

WIC in Collier County

The Florida WIC program, through the Florida Department of Health in Collier County, provides nutritious foods, breastfeeding support, nutrition education, and referrals for eligible pregnant and postpartum women, infants, and children under 5.

The official Collier WIC number is 239-252-8208. The state WIC office number is 1-800-342-3556.

(239) 252-8208

WIC in Collier County

Food assistance (SNAP)

Florida’s official way to apply for SNAP food assistance and other public benefits is the Department of Children and Families ACCESS Florida portal.

The official SNAP page explains the program and how to apply, check a case, or manage benefits.

Florida SNAP informationACCESS Florida application

Florida Abuse Hotline

The Florida Department of Children and Families operates a 24-hour hotline to report known or suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a child or a vulnerable adult.

The official number is 1-800-96-ABUSE (1-800-962-2873). Call 911 if someone is in immediate danger.

1-800-962-2873

Questions families often ask

Where should a family take a child for emergency or urgent care in Naples?

In a medical emergency, call 911. NCH describes its Pediatric Emergency Department at NCH North Naples Hospital, 11190 Health Park Blvd, as the only 24-hour pediatric emergency department in Collier County, for children from birth to age 18. The official Pediatric ER number is 239-624-9135. For walk-in care, Lee Health Kids’ Urgent Care at 3361 Pine Ridge Rd, Suite 101, Naples, is listed as open 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily, with no appointment needed, for infants through young adults up to age 21. The regional children’s hospital emergency department is at Golisano Children’s Hospital in Fort Myers. Source: NCH Pediatric ER. Source: Lee Health Kids’ Urgent Care. Source: Lee Health Golisano Children’s Hospital Emergency Department.

How do families enroll a child in Collier County Public Schools?

New kindergarten through 12th grade students, and students returning after withdrawing, enroll through the district’s Centralized Online Enrollment system at collierschools.com/enroll. The official enrollment page lists help at 239-377-0547, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Pre-K programs have a separate process through the Pre-K department. Source: Collier County Public Schools enrollment.

Who is eligible for Florida VPK, and how do families apply?

The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida says Voluntary Prekindergarten is a state-funded program open to eligible 4- and 5-year-olds, regardless of family income. A child must be 4 years old by September 1 of the school year, and the family must live in Florida. Families apply on the official Florida Early Learning Family Portal, then take the certificate of eligibility to a chosen VPK provider. A Family Resource Advisor can be reached at 239-935-6100. Source: Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, VPK for parents. Source: Florida Early Learning Family Portal.

How does beach parking work for Collier County residents and visitors?

Collier County’s official beach parking page says county residents can get a beach parking permit with proof of residency, and that visitors who are not eligible for the permit pay a flat $10 per day through Mobile Pay to Park. City of Naples beach ends and Florida State Parks nearby have their own fees and rules. The Naples Beaches Guide on this site gathers those official details. Source: Collier County beach parking information.

How can a family check whether the water is safe at a Naples beach?

The Florida Department of Health posts current bacteria results for Collier County beaches on the Florida Healthy Beaches site. Conditions can change, so families should check that official page before swimming. The National Weather Service also publishes rip-current safety guidance. Source: Florida Healthy Beaches, Collier County. Source: National Weather Service rip current safety.

How do families find a licensed childcare provider in Naples?

Florida’s Department of Children and Families publishes a public search of licensed child care providers. The Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida also offers Child Care Resource & Referral, which can send a customized list of centers, family child care homes, and school-age care. Families can call 239-935-6100 or use the Coalition’s online request form. Source: Florida DCF CARES search. Source: Early Learning Coalition of Southwest Florida, Child Care Resource & Referral.

What is 211, and when should a family call it?

211 is a non-emergency help line. United Way of Collier and the Keys says it connects callers to local services such as food, housing, health programs, and disaster relief. Families can dial 211 or 239-263-4211, or text a ZIP code to 898211. In an emergency, call 911. Source: United Way of Collier and the Keys, 211.

How do families find their hurricane evacuation zone in Collier County?

Collier County Emergency Management’s official page includes a way to determine an evacuation zone and to sign up for the Special Needs Program. The department’s published number is 239-252-4311, also listed as Collier 311, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Official maps and alerts can change, so families should use the county’s current tools rather than an old print map. Source: Collier County Emergency Management.

What is Florida Early Steps?

Early Steps is Florida’s official early intervention program for eligible infants and toddlers, from birth to 36 months, with developmental delays, disabilities, or certain at-risk conditions. A parent, caregiver, or physician can make a referral. The official program number is 1-800-218-0001. Source: Florida Department of Health, Early Steps eligibility and screening.

Where can families find storytimes and toddler activities in Naples?

Collier County Public Library branches host toddler, preschool, and family storytimes. The official events listing has current times for each branch. Some Headquarters Library storytimes use morning-of tickets in the children’s room. Source: Collier County Public Library events listing.
