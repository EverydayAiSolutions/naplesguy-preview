Christmas lights

Naples FL Christmas lights

The brightest display is Johnsonville Night Lights in the Garden at Naples Botanical Garden, open nightly from November 27, 2026, through January 3, 2027, except December 24, 25 and 31. Through December 17 it runs 6–9 PM Sunday through Thursday and 6–10 PM on Fridays and Saturdays, with timed tickets sold by the Garden.

Questions people ask

When is the Naples Christmas parade in 2026?

Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at 6:30 PM in downtown Naples. The route runs from Third Street South and Ninth Avenue South up to Fifth Avenue South and ends at City Hall on Eighth Street South. Parade details.

When is the Naples Christmas boat parade?

Saturday, December 19, 2026, from 6:15 to 8:30 PM on Naples Bay. Viewing spots include the Naples City Dock (ticketed), Naples Landing and Bayview Park. Boat parade details.
