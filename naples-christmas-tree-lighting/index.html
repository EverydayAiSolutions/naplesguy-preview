Tree Lighting & Snowfall Celebration on Third Street South

The City of Naples calls the Third Street South tree lighting the start of Christmas in Naples. Santa arrives on a vintage fire truck, the official tree is lit, and a Naples Snowfall follows.

Restaurants and shops stay open late, and local vendors line the street with North Pole treats and sweets. Dining reservations are highly recommended; Third Street South answers questions at (239) 649-6707. The celebration continues Tuesday, November 24, with North Pole on Third from 5 to 8 PM.

Parking

Parking for the Third Street South tree lighting

Third Street South is a short walk from the downtown public garages on Eighth Street South and the Park Street lot. Tree lighting night is one of the busiest evenings of the year downtown, so plan to park before 5 PM or use rideshare.

  • North Garage400 Eighth Street South, at Fourth Avenue South (entrances off Fourth Avenue South or Eighth Street South)
  • South GarageEighth Street South at Sixth Avenue South, between Fifth and Sixth Avenues South
  • Park Street lotPark Street at Sixth Avenue South, beside the South Garage
  • First Avenue South GarageCity garage opened in 2025 near Gulfshore Playhouse, with 366 spaces
  • ValetSeasonal valet stations run along Fifth Avenue South in the evenings

Rates and time limits are posted at each garage; the first floors of the Eighth Street garages have carried a two-hour limit. Rideshare drop-offs keep you out of the garage lines on busy nights.

Questions people ask

When is the Naples Christmas tree lighting 2026?

Monday, November 23, 2026, from 5:00 to 9:00 PM on Third Street South in Naples, according to the City of Naples.

What happens at the Third Street South tree lighting?

Santa arrives on a vintage fire truck, the official tree is lit, and a Naples Snowfall follows. Restaurants and shops stay open late, and local vendors line the street with North Pole treats and sweets.

Should I make a dinner reservation for the tree lighting?

Yes. The City of Naples says dining reservations are highly recommended. Third Street South answers questions at (239) 649-6707.

Where do I park for the Naples tree lighting?

The downtown public garages on Eighth Street South (at Fourth Avenue South and at Sixth Avenue South) and the Park Street lot at Sixth Avenue South are a short walk from Third Street South. Check posted signs for rates and time limits.

Where else are Christmas trees lit near Naples?

Mercato lights its tree around 7 PM on Wednesday, December 2, during its Tree Lighting Ceremony & Holiday Cirque Experience. Gulf Coast Town Center in Fort Myers holds its tree lighting on Saturday, November 28.
