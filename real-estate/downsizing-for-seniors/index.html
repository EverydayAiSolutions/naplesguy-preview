03Selling the home

The same Florida contract, disclosure, inspection, and closing-cost rules apply as in any other sale. They are explained in Steps to Selling and Closing Costs in Florida.

Two timing issues come up often in a senior move:

  • Write a closing date, and any need to occupy after closing, into the contract. Do not assume you can stay in the house after closing unless the contract says so.
  • If the buyer needs a mortgage, build in time for the Loan Estimate, inspections, and the Closing Disclosure (at least three business days before a mortgage closing). CFPB.

What this means for you. Write the senior living move-in date and the sale closing date into the same conversation. A handshake plan to “stay a few extra weeks” is not a contract term unless it is written down.

04A simple tax-year example

Florida homestead eligibility is tied to January 1. The Collier County Property Appraiser states you must be a permanent resident as of January 1 of the year you apply. If you sell the old house in April and you will not own and occupy a new Florida homestead until later that year, you generally cannot claim a new homestead for the year of the sale. You may be able to pre-file after March 1 for the following year, once you will qualify on the next January 1.

Example: you sell a Naples homestead in April. You rent for several months, then close on a smaller home in November and move in. You would ordinarily file the new homestead application for the next tax year (the January 1 after you move in), not for the year of the April sale. If you will own another homestead, bring official form DR-501T when you file so you can ask to transfer the Save Our Homes difference.

Source: Collier homestead page. Source: Form DR-501T (PDF). Source: Fla. Stat. s. 196.031 (2026).

What this means for you. The month you close is not the month a new homestead starts. If you sell in April, plan on the next January 1 for a new exemption, and use the pre-file window after March 1 if you already know you will qualify.

05Federal tax on the sale of a main home

The Internal Revenue Service explains that if you have a capital gain from the sale of your main home, you may qualify to exclude up to $250,000 of that gain from your income, or up to $500,000 if you file a joint return with your spouse. In general, you must meet an ownership test and a use test: you owned the home for at least 24 months out of the five years before the sale, and you used it as a residence for at least 24 months of those five years. For a joint return, one spouse must meet the ownership test and both spouses must meet the use test. You generally cannot have excluded gain from another home sale in the two years before this sale.

Publication 523 has the worksheets, exceptions (including certain work, health, and unforeseen-event moves, and special rules if you used the home partly as a rental or a business), and reporting rules. If you receive Form 1099-S, or you cannot exclude all of the gain, you must report the sale. This page does not calculate your gain or your tax. It is not tax advice.

Source: IRS Topic no. 701, sale of your home. Source: IRS Publication 523, Selling Your Home.

What this means for you. A long-owned Naples homestead can have a large gain on paper. The IRS exclusion, if you qualify, may shelter part or all of that gain — but only if you read the official tests, including any years the house was rented. Talk with a tax professional about your facts.

06Homestead, portability, and the next address

A homestead exemption does not automatically follow you. If the next home will be a Florida residence you own and occupy as your permanent home, file a new homestead application in that county — in Collier County, in person, generally by March 1 — and ask about portability of the Save Our Homes assessment difference (generally up to $500,000, if you had a homestead in any of the prior three years). Use official form DR-501T. Homestead Exemption guide; s. 193.155, F.S.; Form DR-501T.

If you will still own a homestead in Collier County, also ask the Property Appraiser about additional official exemptions that may apply to you, including the additional senior exemption (age 65 and over, with a 2026 household-income limit of $38,686 for 2025 income) and, for some long-term residents, the long-term residency senior exemption. Those rules are on the official senior citizen exemption page.

If the next address is a rented apartment in an assisted living or similar community, you may not have a new homestead to transfer to. That can affect the tax outcome of the sale and of any home you keep. This page does not calculate a tax result. Ask the property appraiser and a tax professional about your facts. This is not legal or tax advice.

Source: Collier homestead page. Source: Senior citizen exemption. Source: Form DR-501T.

What this means for you. If you are moving from a house in Naples into a smaller home you will own, plan the in-person homestead filing and DR-501T. If you are moving into a rented senior living apartment, do not assume any exemption or portability follows you there.

07A short checklist

A short checklist
  • Read the senior Downsizing & Moving guide alongside this one.
  • Line up the sale closing date with the senior living move-in date in the contract.
  • Deliver any HOA or condo documents the buyer is entitled to.
  • If you will own another Florida home, plan the new homestead filing and portability form.
  • Confirm wire instructions by phone before sale proceeds are sent.
