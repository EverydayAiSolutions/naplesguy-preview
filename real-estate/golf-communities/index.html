Golf Communities in Naples, FL

I’ve been helping families buy and sell homes in Naples for 15 years. I’m a Naples dad, and a lot of people ask me about golf communities — which ones include a membership with the house, which ones are optional, and what to check before you buy.

This page is a simple starting point, written from official club, community, and Paradise Coast websites. Membership rules and wait lists can change, so I’ll help you check the current details.

Quick answers

What bundled golf means

In a bundled community, a golf or social membership comes with the house. You still pay dues. Official sites for clubs such as Vanderbilt, Cypress Woods, Cedar Hammock, Heritage Bay, and Stonebridge describe membership as included with the home. The fine print is in each club’s documents.

Equity vs. a fee club

An equity club may give you a share in the club. Many private clubs now use a non-refundable initiation fee instead. Some official sites, such as TPC Treviso Bay, describe a bundled equity membership with select homes. The current club documents are the only place to confirm the model and any wait list.

What to ask before you buy

Ask if membership is included, optional, or unavailable; what dues and assessments are now; whether there is a wait list; and what happens when you sell. Jeff can help you read the club and HOA papers with you.

Common questions

Golf communities in Naples: questions people ask

What does bundled golf mean in Naples?

In a bundled golf community, a golf or social membership comes with the home. Official club sites for Vanderbilt Country Club, Cypress Woods, Cedar Hammock, Heritage Bay, and Stonebridge, for example, say membership is included with the house. You still pay dues, and the exact rules are in each club’s documents. Jeff can help you read those before you buy.

What is the difference between an equity club and a non-equity club?

Some private clubs sell an equity share in the club. Many clubs now use a non-refundable initiation fee instead, with no ownership stake. The only reliable place to confirm which model a club uses, whether any equity is refundable, and whether there is a wait list is that club’s current membership documents.

What should someone ask before buying in a golf community?

Ask whether a membership is included, optional, or not available with that home; what the current dues and any special assessments are; whether there is a wait list; whether you can live there without joining; and what happens to the membership when you sell. Jeff can help you go through the club documents and any HOA or CDD records. Call or text 609-575-8626.

Are there public golf courses in Naples?

Yes. Official club and Paradise Coast pages describe public or daily-fee play at places such as Arrowhead, Hibiscus, Valencia, Panther Run in Ave Maria, Lely Resort’s Mustang and Flamingo courses, and TPC Treviso Bay. Hours, tee times, and seasonal limits change, so they should be checked on the official site.

Do I have to play golf to live in a golf community?

It depends on the community. In some bundled or deeded clubs, a membership comes with the home, though it may be a social membership rather than full golf. In other places, golf is optional or is a separate private club with no homes on the course. The Country Club of Naples, CAVU, and Hideout, for example, describe themselves as clubs without homes tied to every membership. Always confirm the rule for the exact address.
