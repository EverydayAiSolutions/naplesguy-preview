Collier County tools

Three official tools to use first

Start with these county pages before you tour a house in Naples, Marco Island, or unincorporated Collier County. Each link opens in a new tab.

1. Parcel search

Collier County Property Appraiser

Type the street address into the official parcel search. You will see who owns it, the assessed and taxable values, and whether a homestead or other exemption is already on the roll.

What this means for you. Use this on any house you are serious about in Collier County — including the City of Naples and Marco Island — so you are looking at the county’s numbers, not only a listing flyer.

2. Recording fees

Collier Clerk deed calculator · mortgage calculator

The Clerk of the Circuit Court records the deed (and any mortgage) and collects documentary stamp tax and intangible tax. The official calculators run those figures for you.

What this means for you. Open the calculator when you are comparing cash versus a loan. A cash deal still has deed stamps and recording; a loan adds mortgage stamps and intangible tax.

3. Flood map

Collier 2024 flood map · Floodplain office

Search the address on the county’s official February 8, 2024 flood map. The same tool lets you check whether an elevation certificate is already on file. The floodplain office can answer map questions.

What this means for you. Do this before you finalize an offer, especially near water in Naples, Marco Island, or along a canal. The zone on the map — not a neighbor’s memory — is what lenders and insurers use.

Naples Neighborhood

Old Naples

Old Naples is a neighborhood in the City of Naples. Part of the area, generally from 9th to 13th Avenues South between 3rd Street and the Gulf of Mexico, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the Naples Historic District.

Port Royal

Port Royal is a neighborhood in the City of Naples, on the south end of the city along Naples Bay. In the early 1950s, John Glenn Sample began dredging marshland here to create peninsulas and canals with lots overlooking the bay, and named the project Port Royal.

Aqualane Shores

Aqualane Shores is a neighborhood in the City of Naples, officially shown on the city’s neighborhood map. City stormwater documents describe the area as mainly residential land, generally between 15th and 21st Avenues South, from Naples Bay to the Gulf of Mexico.

Park Shore

Park Shore is a neighborhood in the City of Naples, shown on the city’s official neighborhood map. City records describe Park Shore planned-development units along Gulf Shore Boulevard and Venetian Bay, including a public beach access at Horizon Way.

Pelican Bay

Pelican Bay is a census-designated place in unincorporated Collier County, in the North Naples area. It is bordered by the City of Naples to the south, U.S. 41 (the Tamiami Trail) to the east, Vanderbilt Beach Road to the north, and the Gulf of Mexico to the west; the 2020 census counted 6,660 residents.

Vanderbilt Beach

Vanderbilt Beach is an unincorporated community in Collier County, on the Gulf of Mexico north of Pelican Bay and west of Naples Park, about 9 miles north of the center of Naples. Collier County operates Vanderbilt Beach Park, a public beach access at 100 Vanderbilt Beach Road.

North Naples

North Naples is an unincorporated community in northwest Collier County, immediately north of the City of Naples. The area includes the census-designated places of Naples Park, Pelican Bay, and Pine Ridge, and is part of the Naples–Marco Island metropolitan area.

Golden Gate

Golden Gate is a census-designated place in unincorporated Collier County, about 8 miles northeast of Naples. The U.S. Census Bureau recorded 25,321 residents in 2020 in a roughly 2-by-2-mile area of higher-density development than the land around it.

East Naples

East Naples is an unincorporated community immediately east of the City of Naples and the county seat of Collier County. County offices moved here from Everglades City in 1962, when a new courthouse opened.

Naples Park

Naples Park is an unincorporated census-designated place in the North Naples area of Collier County, just north of Pelican Bay. It opened in 1951; the 2020 census counted 5,092 residents, and the CDP is generally bounded by U.S. 41, 111th Avenue North, Vanderbilt Drive, and 91st Avenue North.

Marco Island

Marco Island is an incorporated city in Collier County, about 20 miles south of Naples, and the largest barrier island in Southwest Florida’s Ten Thousand Islands area. The city reincorporated on August 28, 1997, and had 15,760 residents at the 2020 census.

Estero / Bonita Springs

Estero is an incorporated village in Lee County, just north of the Collier County line and the City of Bonita Springs; it became a village on December 31, 2014, and had 36,939 residents in 2020. Bonita Springs, also in Lee County, reincorporated as a city on December 31, 1999, had 53,644 residents in 2020, and sits on Estero Bay and the Gulf of Mexico.

Orange Tree / Ave Maria

Orangetree and Ave Maria are census-designated places in unincorporated Collier County, northeast of Naples. Orangetree is about 23 miles northeast of downtown Naples and had 5,896 residents in 2020. Ave Maria, a planned community and the home of Ave Maria University, is about 36 miles northeast of downtown Naples and had 6,242 residents in 2020.
