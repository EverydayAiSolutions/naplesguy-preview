Before you buy

What to ask in any community

Association documents

  • Ask for the HOA disclosure summary Florida requires for homes in mandatory associations (s. 720.401).
  • For a condo, ask for the documents the seller must provide, including milestone inspection and reserve study reports where they apply (s. 718.503, s. 553.899).
  • Read the master and neighborhood budgets, and ask about planned special assessments.

Clubs and rules

  • Confirm in writing whether any club membership is tied to the address.
  • Get current initiation, dues and minimums from the club itself.
  • Ask about rental, pet, vehicle and lease rules before you sign.

The house itself

  • Get flood and wind insurance quotes early, using the elevation certificate.
  • Book a full inspection; older homes often also need 4-point and wind-mitigation reports for insurance.
  • Check the tax bill for any district (CDD) line.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Which Naples communities have optional golf?

In these guides, golf or club membership is a separate choice in The Vineyards, Pelican Marsh, Tiburón, Kensington, Imperial Golf Estates, Shadow Wood, The Quarry, Fiddler's Creek and Gateway, and two of Lely Resort's three courses are public. Golf is bundled with the home at Heritage Bay, Stonebridge and Copperleaf, in The Brooks, and membership is required at Quail West, Wyndemere and TwinEagles. Always confirm for the specific address.

Which communities have their own beach access?

Bay Colony has a beach club on two miles of beach, The Club at Mediterra has a beach club, Pelican Landing has a 34-acre beach island reached by shuttle boat, The Commons Club at The Brooks has a private beach club on Little Hickory Island, The Moorings has a Beach Park for association members, and Fiddler's Creek offers beach access through its Tarpon Club.

How do I find the schools for a home?

Use the official lookup for the county: Collier County Public Schools' attendance boundaries or the School District of Lee County's school zones. Assignments go by address and can change.

How do I check a home's flood zone?

Search the address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center and ask the seller for an elevation certificate. Get insurance quotes before your inspection period ends.
