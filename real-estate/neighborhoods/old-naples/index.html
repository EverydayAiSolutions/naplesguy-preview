Overview

About Old Naples

Old Naples is a neighborhood in the City of Naples. Part of the area, generally from 9th to 13th Avenues South between 3rd Street and the Gulf of Mexico, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the Naples Historic District.

Parks & beaches

Public places nearby, from official listings

  • Cambier Park

    A City of Naples park at 755 8th Avenue South, just south of Fifth Avenue South in downtown Naples.

    Source: City of Naples Parks & Recreation

  • Naples Pier

    A City of Naples park at 25 12th Avenue South, at the Gulf. The City says the pier is currently closed for a rebuild and is scheduled to reopen in July 2027. Pedestrian beach access at Broad Avenue South and 13th Avenue South is described on the City’s rebuild page.

    Source: City of Naples Parks & Recreation
