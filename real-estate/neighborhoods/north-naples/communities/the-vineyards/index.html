The community

About The Vineyards

The Vineyards is in North Naples, ZIP 34119, along Vanderbilt Beach Road. The Vineyards Community Association describes 38 separate neighborhoods with villas, condos, coach homes, single-family homes and custom homes, reached through northern and southern gated entrances.

At its center is Vineyards Country Club, founded in 1988, with North and South 18-hole courses. Club membership is its own decision, and the club says Full Golf memberships are open to residents and non-residents.

Golf, clubs and the HOA

How membership works

Vineyards Country Club is a private, family-owned, non-equity club founded in 1988. It offers two membership types, Full Golf and Lifestyle, and states that Full Golf memberships are open to residents and non-residents of the community.

Owning a home in The Vineyards and joining the club are two separate decisions. Ask the club for current initiation and dues in writing.

Trade-offs to weigh

  • Lower flood share. ZIP 34119 is rated minor for flood (17%), lower than most Naples ZIPs. Source: Redfin: 34119 housing market (Oct 2026)

Fees, dues and assessments change every year, and club figures reported by third parties can differ from the club's own schedule. Confirm every number with the association estoppel, the club and the county tax bill before you make an offer.

Schools

Which schools is a home zoned for?

Collier County Public Schools assigns students by home address, and boundaries can change from year to year. Enter the exact address in the district's official tool to see the zoned elementary, middle and high schools.

Flood zone

Check the flood zone by address

Flood zones are set parcel by parcel, and two homes on the same street can differ. Look up the exact address on FEMA's map, ask the seller for an elevation certificate, and get flood and wind insurance quotes before your inspection period ends.

Closing costs

Closing costs in plain terms

Closing costs in Florida come in a few clear pieces. Some are state taxes with fixed rates, some are set by rule, and the rest come from your lender, the county and the association. Who pays which item is decided in the purchase contract.

Deed stamp tax

A state tax on the deed: 70 cents for every $100 of the price.

Florida Statutes 201.02 (deed stamps) · Florida Department of Revenue: documentary stamp tax

Mortgage stamp tax

If you borrow: 35 cents for every $100 of the loan.

Florida Statutes 201.08 (note and mortgage stamps)

Intangible tax

If you borrow: 0.2% of the loan amount, paid once.

Florida Statutes 199.133 (intangible tax on mortgages)

Owner's title insurance

Protects the owner against title problems. Florida sets the premium by rule, so it does not vary by company.

Florida Administrative Code 69O-186.003 (title insurance rates)

Recording fees

The county clerk charges to record the deed and mortgage.

Collier Clerk: deed fee calculator

Association items

Estoppel certificate, application or transfer fees, and any capital contribution. Ask each association for its current amounts.

Taxes and assessments

Property taxes are split by date at closing. Check the tax bill for any district (CDD) assessment.

Collier County Property Appraiser

Loan costs

Lender fees, prepaid interest, and escrows for taxes and insurance. All of it appears on your Closing Disclosure.

Before you buy

What to ask before an offer

Association documents

  • Ask for the HOA disclosure summary Florida requires for homes in mandatory associations (s. 720.401).
  • For a condo, ask for the documents the seller must provide, including milestone inspection and reserve study reports where they apply (s. 718.503, s. 553.899).
  • Read the master and neighborhood budgets, and ask about planned special assessments.

Clubs and rules

  • Confirm in writing whether any club membership is tied to the address.
  • Get current initiation, dues and minimums from the club itself.
  • Ask about rental, pet, vehicle and lease rules before you sign.

The house itself

  • Get flood and wind insurance quotes early, using the elevation certificate.
  • Book a full inspection; older homes often also need 4-point and wind-mitigation reports for insurance.
  • Check the tax bill for any district (CDD) line.

Questions people ask

Is golf membership required in The Vineyards?

No. Vineyards Country Club is a separate, private, non-equity club. It offers Full Golf and Lifestyle memberships and says Full Golf memberships are open to residents and non-residents.

How many neighborhoods are in The Vineyards?

Thirty-eight, according to the Vineyards Community Association, with villas, condos, coach homes, single-family homes and custom homes.

Is The Vineyards gated?

The community has northern and southern gated entrances, and some neighborhoods inside have their own gates.

How many golf holes does The Vineyards have?

Thirty-six: the North and South courses at Vineyards Country Club.
