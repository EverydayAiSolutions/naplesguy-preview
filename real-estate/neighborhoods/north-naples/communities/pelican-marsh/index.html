The community

About Pelican Marsh

Pelican Marsh is in North Naples, ZIP 34109, about a mile from Mercato. The Foundation of Pelican Marsh says there are 26 named neighborhoods of condominiums and single-family homes, with 133 acres of nature preserves including the Cocohatchee Strand.

The community center belongs to the residents through the Foundation, and Pelican Marsh Golf Club is a private club inside the gates with its own membership.

Amenities

Life in Pelican Marsh

The Foundation's Community Center lists 8 Har-Tru tennis courts and 6 pickleball courts, all lighted, with a pro shop, clinics, leagues and round robins. There is also a fitness center with trainers, a resident library and computer room, and a social program of trips and events.

Mature landscaping and preserves give the community a lush, settled feel, with eagles and wading birds in the preserves.

Golf, clubs and the HOA

How membership works

Pelican Marsh Golf Club is a private membership club with an Audubon-certified 18-hole course. The club states that residency is not required for membership, and owning a home does not make you a member.

The Community Center is run by the Foundation of Pelican Marsh, with a board elected by residents.

Trade-offs to weigh

  • Golf optional. You can live here without joining the golf club, but the club has a long wait list if you want in later. Source: Domain Realty Group: golf club membership and initiation fees (data as of Nov 4, 2026)
  • Central North Naples. Close to U.S. 41, Airport-Pulling Road and shopping; not on the beach. Source: Pelican Marsh Community Development District
  • Flood. First Street rates ZIP 34109 “Moderate” (77%). Check the exact lot. Sources: Redfin: 34109 housing market (Oct 2026); Collier County 2024 flood map (DFIRM) lookup

Fees, dues and assessments change every year, and club figures reported by third parties can differ from the club's own schedule. Confirm every number with the association estoppel, the club and the county tax bill before you make an offer.

Closing costs

Closing costs in plain terms

Closing costs in Florida come in a few clear pieces. Some are state taxes with fixed rates, some are set by rule, and the rest come from your lender, the county and the association. Who pays which item is decided in the purchase contract.

Deed stamp tax

A state tax on the deed: 70 cents for every $100 of the price.

Florida Statutes 201.02 (deed stamps) · Florida Department of Revenue: documentary stamp tax

Mortgage stamp tax

If you borrow: 35 cents for every $100 of the loan.

Florida Statutes 201.08 (note and mortgage stamps)

Intangible tax

If you borrow: 0.2% of the loan amount, paid once.

Florida Statutes 199.133 (intangible tax on mortgages)

Owner's title insurance

Protects the owner against title problems. Florida sets the premium by rule, so it does not vary by company.

Florida Administrative Code 69O-186.003 (title insurance rates)

Recording fees

The county clerk charges to record the deed and mortgage.

Collier Clerk: deed fee calculator

Association items

Estoppel certificate, application or transfer fees, and any capital contribution. Ask each association for its current amounts.

Taxes and assessments

Property taxes are split by date at closing. Check the tax bill for any district (CDD) assessment.

Before you buy

What to ask before an offer

Association documents

  • Ask for the HOA disclosure summary Florida requires for homes in mandatory associations (s. 720.401).
  • For a condo, ask for the documents the seller must provide, including milestone inspection and reserve study reports where they apply (s. 718.503, s. 553.899).
  • Read the master and neighborhood budgets, and ask about planned special assessments.

Clubs and rules

  • Confirm in writing whether any club membership is tied to the address.
  • Get current initiation, dues and minimums from the club itself.
  • Ask about rental, pet, vehicle and lease rules before you sign.

The house itself

  • Get flood and wind insurance quotes early, using the elevation certificate.
  • Book a full inspection; older homes often also need 4-point and wind-mitigation reports for insurance.
  • Check the tax bill for any district (CDD) line.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do you have to join the golf club to live in Pelican Marsh?

No. Pelican Marsh Golf Club is a private membership club, and the club says residency is not a requirement for membership. The resident Community Center is separate.

How many neighborhoods are in Pelican Marsh?

Twenty-six named neighborhoods of condominiums and single-family homes, according to the Foundation of Pelican Marsh.

How far is Pelican Marsh from the beach?

The Foundation says about one and a half miles from the area's beaches. Vanderbilt Beach and Clam Pass Park are each roughly 2.5 to 3.5 miles away in a straight line.

What does the Pelican Marsh Community Center have?

Eight Har-Tru tennis courts and six pickleball courts, all lighted, a fitness center, a library and computer room, and a social program.
