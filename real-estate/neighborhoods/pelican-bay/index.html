Overview

What Pelican Bay is like

Pelican Bay is a census-designated place in unincorporated Collier County, between the City of Naples, U.S. 41, Vanderbilt Beach Road and the Gulf. WCI was the master developer and set aside common areas to be kept up forever under the Pelican Bay Foundation, the master homeowners association. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation

It is mostly mid- and high-rise condo towers along the Gulf and the mangroves, with villas and single-family neighborhoods behind them. The president of the Pelican Bay Property Owners Association describes 92 residential and mid- or high-rise neighborhoods. Bay Colony, a gated enclave, is inside Pelican Bay. Source: Gulfshore Business: Neighbors band in opposition to Naples Grande water park proposal (Mar 2026)

Membership in the Foundation is required for every owner. It runs nearly three miles of staffed beach with loungers, cabanas and kayaks, the Marker 36 and Sandbar beach restaurants, a tram to the beach, a fitness center, and 26 tennis and 20 pickleball courts. Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation; Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)

Buying or selling

Buying or selling in Pelican Bay

Buying here?

  • Price range. The ZIP 34108 median was $993,491 for the three months to October 2026, but that ZIP also covers Vanderbilt Beach and Naples Park. Use Pelican Bay sales of the same building or neighborhood. Source: Redfin: 34108 housing market (Oct 2026)
  • Budget for fees. Add the Pelican Bay Foundation fee ($3,435 a year, FY2027), the county PBSD assessment ($1,250 per unit, FY2027), and a one-time $15,000 resale fee paid by the buyer, plus your condo or neighborhood association dues. Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF); Collier County: Pelican Bay Services Division
  • Older towers. Many buildings are older high-rises. Check Collier County’s milestone list and DBPR’s SIRS filings, and ask about special assessments. Sources: Collier County Milestone Inspections (map and status lookup); Florida DBPR: SIRS Reporting and Database (condos)

Selling here?

  • Market speed. Homes in 34108 took a median 117 days to sell. Price against recent Pelican Bay sales, not the ZIP median. Source: Redfin: 34108 housing market (Oct 2026)
  • What buyers here value. The Foundation’s staffed beaches, tram, dining and courts are the draw, and buyers will total up the Foundation, PBSD and $15,000 resale fees when comparing. Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation; Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)
  • What to prepare. Order the Foundation estoppel early and gather your association’s budget and insurance details. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)
  • Flood disclosure. Florida sellers of residential property must give buyers a written flood disclosure at or before signing the contract, covering known flood damage, flood insurance claims and federal flood aid. Source: Florida Statutes 689.302: flood disclosure in sales of residential property
  • Condo documents. Condo sellers must give buyers the association documents Florida requires, including the milestone inspection summary and the structural integrity reserve study when the building needs them. Get these from the association early. Source: Florida Statutes 718.503: condo resale disclosures, incl. milestone and SIRS

From residents and local news

What locals like and complain about

Paraphrased from the linked news stories and community groups. No quotes are made up.

What people like

  • The beach setup: staffed Foundation beaches with dining, rentals and a tram from the neighborhoods. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation
  • An active racquets and social scene, which the Foundation calls one of the most active racquets communities in Southwest Florida. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation
  • Shopping and culture close by: Waterside Shops and Artis—Naples sit on Pelican Bay’s edge. Sources: Waterside Shops; Artis—Naples

Common complaints

  • Commercial pressure on a residential area. In 2026 residents packed meetings against a proposed water-park-style addition at the Naples Grande resort, worried about noise, traffic and parking; the Pelican Bay Property Owners Association said the fight “will most likely end up in the courts.” Source: Gulfshore Business: Neighbors band in opposition to Naples Grande water park proposal (Mar 2026)
  • Rising and stacked fees. The county Pelican Bay Services Division assessment reached $1,550 per unit for FY2026, more than half of it capital money, then dropped to $1,250 for FY2027. It comes on top of Foundation and condo fees. Sources: Collier County FY2026 first budget hearing packet, incl. Pelican Bay assessment roll (PDF); Collier County: Pelican Bay Services Division
  • Hurricane recovery. The Foundation’s FY2026 budget was its first at full capacity in all service areas after several years of partial operations caused by hurricanes. Source: Pelican Bay Post, September 2025 (Foundation FY2026 budget article)

A good fit for

  • People who want beach access, dining and tennis without joining a golf club. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation
  • Condo buyers who want a high-rise on the Gulf or the mangroves. Source: Gulfshore Business: Neighbors band in opposition to Naples Grande water park proposal (Mar 2026)
  • Seasonal owners who want a full-service, lock-and-leave setup. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation

Probably not for

  • Buyers who want no association fees at all. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)
  • People who want a big yard or a lot of land. Source: Gulfshore Business: Neighbors band in opposition to Naples Grande water park proposal (Mar 2026)
  • Anyone not ready to read condo reserve studies and milestone reports on an older tower. Source: Collier County Milestone Inspections (map and status lookup)
Trade-offs

How Pelican Bay compares

Fees vs. Old Naples

Every Pelican Bay owner pays the Foundation ($3,435 a year in FY2027) and the county PBSD assessment ($1,250 per unit in FY2027), plus a one-time $15,000 buyer resale fee. Old Naples single-family homes have no master association. Condo fees come on top of both.

Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF); Collier County: Pelican Bay Services Division; Old Naples Association

What the fees buy

Foundation fees pay for staffed beaches, dining, the tram, courts and fitness. The PBSD pays for the water management system, medians and landscaping, Clam Bay and mangrove care, U.S. 41 berms and beach renourishment reserves.

Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation; Collier County FY2026 first budget hearing packet, incl. Pelican Bay assessment roll (PDF)

Price

The 34108 median ($993,491) is far below Old Naples, but it blends Pelican Bay with Vanderbilt Beach and Naples Park, so Pelican Bay itself can price differently. Use solds from the specific building or street.

Source: Redfin: 34108 housing market (Oct 2026)

Storm exposure

The National Hurricane Center recorded 8 to 12 feet of surge above ground in North Naples during Ian, with the worst impacts on the immediate coastline.

Source: National Hurricane Center: Hurricane Ian report (2023)

Flood, storms & insurance

Flood zones, evacuation and insurance

These are the biggest cost and risk questions for most buyers here. Check each address.

Flood zone

Check the exact address on Collier County’s 2024 flood map or FEMA’s map center, and ask for the elevation certificate. Collier’s 90-day appeal period for proposed new maps began August 19, 2026.

Sources: Collier County 2024 flood map (DFIRM) lookup; FEMA Flood Map Service Center; Collier County Floodplain Management

Evacuation zone

Pelican Bay is on the Gulf. Look up your zone on Know Your Zone and follow Collier County Emergency Management for orders.

Sources: Florida Division of Emergency Management: Know Your Zone; Collier County Emergency Management

Flood insurance

Unincorporated Collier County is a CRS Class 5 community, which lowers NFIP premiums. Condo buildings carry a master flood policy; ask what it covers and what the unit owner must insure.

Sources: Collier County Floodplain Management; FloodSmart (National Flood Insurance Program)

Condo safety rules

Condo buildings three stories or taller need milestone inspections (25 years old within 3 miles of salt water) and structural integrity reserve studies. Collier County publishes a milestone map and status lookup.

Sources: Collier County Milestone Inspections (map and status lookup); Fla. Stat. 553.899 (milestone inspections); Florida DBPR: SIRS Reporting and Database (condos)

Public documents we found

  • Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel for FY2027: $3,435 a year ($2,180 replacement reserve, $497 operating, $758 bulk media), $858.75 a quarter, and the $15,000 resale capital assessment. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)
  • Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel for FY2026: $3,295 a year and the $10,000 resale fee that applied before October 1, 2026, plus the one-time exemption for owners moving within Pelican Bay. Source: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2026 (PDF)
  • Collier County’s FY2026 assessment roll for the Pelican Bay unit: $768.70 operating plus $781.30 capital per unit, on 7,659.90 assessable units. Source: Collier County FY2026 first budget hearing packet, incl. Pelican Bay assessment roll (PDF)
  • Pelican Bay Services Division monthly board packets with financial statements (for example, $10.8 million cash on hand on Feb. 28, 2026). Source: Pelican Bay Services Division board packet, April 8, 2026 (PDF)
  • Collier County milestone inspection map and building status lookup for each tower. Source: Collier County Milestone Inspections (map and status lookup)

Not public: ask for these

  • The Foundation’s full audited financials and reserve study were not posted publicly that we could find; the estoppel and the Pelican Bay Post budget summary are public.
  • Each condo association’s budget, reserve study and SIRS are posted for owners behind a login, as Florida law allows. Request them through the seller or listing agent.

Questions to ask the condo or HOA

  • From the Foundation: the current budget, latest audit and reserve study, and any planned capital projects (beach facility work, tram changes).
  • From the condo association: budget, reserve study, SIRS, milestone reports, special assessments, insurance and deductibles.
  • Whether the condo association already pays the Foundation fee inside the monthly dues (for condos it does; single-family owners pay the Foundation directly).
  • Rental minimums and how many leases a year are allowed.
Before you buy

Questions to ask before you buy in Pelican Bay

  • What are the total yearly costs: condo or HOA dues, Foundation fee, PBSD assessment, and insurance?
  • Is the $15,000 resale capital assessment due, or do I qualify for the one-time exemption?
  • For a tower: when was the milestone inspection, what did it find, and is the SIRS fully funded?
  • What happened to this building in Ian (2022) and Milton (2024)?
  • What is the flood zone, and what does the master flood policy cover?
  • Which beach access and tram stop is closest, and how busy is it in season?
FAQ

Pelican Bay questions people ask

How much are Pelican Bay fees?

For FY2027 the Pelican Bay Foundation assessment is $3,435 a year per unit ($858.75 a quarter), and the county Pelican Bay Services Division assessment is $1,250 per unit on the property tax bill. Buyers also pay a one-time $15,000 resale capital assessment for deeds recorded on or after October 1, 2026. Condo dues are separate.

Sources: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF); Collier County: Pelican Bay Services Division; Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2026 (PDF)
Is Pelican Bay Foundation membership required?

Yes. The Foundation’s estoppel states that membership is required of all property owners in Pelican Bay. For condos the association pays the Foundation bill; single-family owners pay it directly.

Source: Pelican Bay Foundation estoppel, FY2027 (PDF)
Is Pelican Bay gated?

Bay Colony, inside Pelican Bay, is a gated enclave. Gate and front-desk arrangements vary by building and neighborhood, so ask about the specific one.

Source: Gulfshore Business: Neighbors band in opposition to Naples Grande water park proposal (Mar 2026)
What does the Pelican Bay Services Division do?

It is a Collier County taxing unit that maintains Pelican Bay’s water management system, medians and landscaping, Clam Bay and the mangroves, and sets aside capital money for berms, signs, entrances and beach renourishment.

Source: Collier County FY2026 first budget hearing packet, incl. Pelican Bay assessment roll (PDF)
What do homes cost in Pelican Bay?

Redfin’s median for ZIP 34108, which also includes Vanderbilt Beach and Naples Park, was $993,491 for the three months ending October 2026. Pelican Bay prices vary widely by building.

Source: Redfin: 34108 housing market (Oct 2026)
