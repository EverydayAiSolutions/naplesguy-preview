Before you buy in Belize: fees, lender rules and true cost

What buyers, agents and mortgage lenders check. Every figure links to its source; anything that isn’t public is in “Questions to ask”.

Condo safety & lender eligibility

  • Collier County’s milestone map and Florida DBPR’s SIRS list show inspection and reserve-study status by building. Sources: Collier County Milestone Inspections (map and status lookup); Florida DBPR: SIRS Reporting and Database (condos)
Lender red flags, in plain English
  • Litigation: Fannie Mae will not buy loans in projects where the association is in pending litigation over safety, structural soundness, habitability or functional use (minor cases can be excepted). Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.1-03: Ineligible projects
  • Critical repairs: Projects needing critical repairs are ineligible, including unfunded repairs over $10,000 per unit due within 12 months, or an evacuation order. Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.1-03: Ineligible projects
  • Reserves under 10%: Fannie Mae’s full review needs a budget that puts at least 10% toward replacement reserves (or an acceptable reserve study). FHA needs reserves of at least 10% of 12 months of assessments. Sources: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.2-02: Full review process (reserves, delinquencies); HUD Single Family Housing Policy Handbook 4000.1 (condo project standards) (PDF)
  • Delinquent dues: Fannie Mae and FHA both cap owners behind on dues at 15% of units (60+ days for Fannie Mae). Sources: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.2-02: Full review process (reserves, delinquencies); HUD Single Family Housing Policy Handbook 4000.1 (condo project standards) (PDF)
  • Owner-occupancy: FHA generally wants at least 50% of units owner-occupied in existing projects, and insures no more than 50% of units. Sources: HUD: FHA issues new condominium approval rule (2019); HUD Single Family Housing Policy Handbook 4000.1 (condo project standards) (PDF)
  • Single-entity ownership: Fannie Mae treats a project of 21 or more units as ineligible if one person or company owns more than 20% of the units. Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.1-03: Ineligible projects
  • Special assessments: Lenders must review each special assessment’s purpose, amount, balance left and payoff date; one tied to an unfixed critical repair makes the project ineligible. Source: Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.1-03: Ineligible projects

Mortgage & lender checklist

  1. Check whether the condo is on HUD’s FHA-approved list.HUD: FHA-approved condominium lookup
  2. Get the association’s answers on reserves, delinquencies, litigation, single-entity ownership and critical repairs.Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.2-02: Full review process (reserves, delinquencies) · Fannie Mae Selling Guide B4-2.1-03: Ineligible projects
  3. For buildings three stories or taller: the milestone inspection report and the SIRS.Fla. Stat. 553.899 (milestone inspections) · Fla. Stat. 718.112 (condo bylaws: budgets, reserves, SIRS)
  4. Look up the flood zone and get a flood insurance quote before you lock a rate.FEMA Flood Map Service Center · FloodSmart (National Flood Insurance Program)
  5. Get homeowners and wind insurance quotes early.Citizens Property Insurance Corporation · Florida Office of Insurance Regulation
  6. Give your lender every yearly assessment: HOA, CDD and county assessments from the tax bill.Collier County Tax Collector: Property tax search (your parcel’s millage and assessments)

Buyer due-diligence checklist

  1. Pull the Property Appraiser record: owner, year built, recorded sales.Collier County Property Appraiser
  2. Read the real tax bill, including CDD and other non-ad valorem lines.Collier County Tax Collector: Property tax search (your parcel’s millage and assessments)
  3. Check permit history and open permits.Collier County permit history and permit search (GMCD portal) · City of Naples Building: Citizen Self Serve permit search
  4. Check code enforcement cases.Collier County CityView portal (permits, inspections, code cases)
  5. Search recorded liens, judgments and the declaration.Collier County Clerk: Official Records search (deeds, declarations, plats)
  6. Read the flood disclosure and check the FEMA zone.Florida Statutes 689.302: flood disclosure in sales of residential property · FEMA Flood Map Service Center
  7. Get the HOA disclosure summary and the CDD notice.Fla. Stat. 720.401 (HOA disclosure summary for buyers) · Fla. Stat. 190.048 (CDD disclosure to buyers)
  8. For a condo: the resale disclosures, including milestone and SIRS.Florida Statutes 718.503: condo resale disclosures, incl. milestone and SIRS
  9. Confirm the association is active on Sunbiz.Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz): search by entity name
  10. Confirm zoned schools for the address.Collier County Public Schools: attendance boundaries

Questions to ask (not public online)

  • HOA, master and sub-association dues, reserves and any special assessment.
  • CDD operations and debt service, and the payoff year.
  • Minimum lease term, pet rules and whether the association approves buyers.
  • Club membership: required or optional, initiation and dues.
  • Will your lender use Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or FHA rules for this property, and does the project pass?
  • Is the community age-restricted (55+)? Check the declaration.

Property & taxes

  • Collier County Property Appraiser: Real Property Search (parcels and sales)

    Look up any parcel by owner, address, or subdivision/condo name. Each record shows values, building details, exemptions and recorded sales with dates and prices.

  • Property Appraiser GIS parcel map

    Interactive map with parcel lines, aerial photos, subdivisions and a sales layer. Good for seeing what sold nearby.

  • Property Appraiser: Notice of Proposed Property Taxes (TRIM)

    Explains the August TRIM notice. To see one for a home, open its parcel record and choose the Trims tab.

  • Property Appraiser: Homestead exemption

    Who qualifies, the March 1 deadline and what to bring. A buyer’s taxes can differ from the seller’s if the homestead goes away.

  • Property Appraiser: Data downloads (countywide sales and parcel files)

    Countywide sales, parcel and building files you can download as spreadsheets to build your own comps.

  • Collier County Tax Collector: Property tax search

    The real tax bill for any parcel, including CDD and other non-ad valorem assessments, and whether it’s paid.

  • Tax Collector: Property tax due dates and payment options

    When bills go out, early-payment discounts and installment plans.

Ownership & liens

  • Collier Clerk: Official Records search (deeds, mortgages, liens)

    Search by name, instrument number or date for deeds, mortgages, liens, lis pendens, satisfactions, condo declarations and plats.

  • Collier Clerk: Records search hub (court records, foreclosures, tax deeds)

    Links to civil and probate court records, foreclosure sale lists, tax deed auctions and Value Adjustment Board records.

  • Collier Clerk: Recording fees and documentary stamp rates

    Doc stamps are $0.70 per $100 on deeds; mortgages are $0.35 per $100 plus $2 per $1,000 intangible tax.

  • Collier Clerk: Mortgage fee calculator

    Enter the loan amount to estimate doc stamps, intangible tax and recording fees.

  • Sunbiz: Florida corporation and association search

    Find an HOA, condo association or LLC’s officers, registered agent and annual reports.
