55+ and Active Adult Communities in Naples

Naples-area 55+ communities at a glance, what “55+” really means, and what to ask before you buy.

Quick answers
  • What 55+ means. Under federal Housing for Older Persons rules, a community may qualify as 55-or-older housing if it is intended and operated for people 55 and older, at least 80 percent of occupied homes have at least one resident who is 55 or older, and it publishes and follows policies that show that intent. Source: 42 U.S.C. 3607 and HUD’s 24 CFR 100.304 and 24 CFR 100.305.
  • It is not always “everyone is 55.” The federal 80 percent rule leaves room for some occupied homes without a resident who is 55, unless the community writes a stricter rule of its own. HUD also requires written policies and a way to verify ages, including surveys at least every two years. Source: 24 CFR 100.306 and 24 CFR 100.307.
  • HOA and co-op fees vary. This page does not invent a typical monthly fee. Ask the association or builder for the current budget and fee schedule, and read the official Florida disclosure package. Enchanting Shores publishes a quarterly assessment figure on its official FAQ; Hitching Post publishes current monthly fees on its official About page. Confirm both with the office. See HOA & condo rules.
  • This is independent homeownership, not a care contract. A 55+ neighborhood is not the same as licensed assisted living or a continuing-care campus. If care may be needed later, that plan should be made separately. See Senior Care.

Communities verified from official sources

This list includes only communities whose official builder, association, or community site says they are 55+, over 55, or housing for older persons. It is not every age-restricted address in Collier County, and it is not a ranking. Photos are licensed representative images. They are not pictures of the named communities.

Builder 55+ and active-adult communities

These are the larger for-sale 55+ neighborhoods in the Naples area whose official builder sites describe an active-adult program.

Del Webb Naples

Location
Ave Maria, eastern Collier County (ZIP 34142)
Home types
New single-family homes and villas, listed on the official Del Webb page.
Amenities
Two clubhouses, a resort pool with beach entry and a lap pool, a fitness center and movement studio, tennis, pickleball, bocce, and optional membership at the on-site 18-hole Panther Run Golf Club. A lifestyle director is also listed.
Age restriction
Age-restricted 55+ community. Ave Maria’s official FAQ says the larger town is for all ages, and that Del Webb Naples is the only age-restricted neighborhood there, for qualifying residents 55 and older.
Good to know
Del Webb Naples sits inside Ave Maria. It is not a second, separate “Del Webb Ave Maria” community.

Official site: Del Webb Naples official page · Ave Maria FAQ.

Valencia Sky

Location
East Naples (ZIP 34112). The official sales address is 2841 Ridgecrest Place, Naples, FL 34112, about five miles from downtown Naples.
Home types
New homes by GL Homes. The official page invites buyers to tour decorated model homes.
Amenities
GL Homes lists a staffed gate, a lifestyle director, yard and common-area maintenance, and no community development district (CDD) fees. The Community Center and Sports & Racquet Club are described as open, with a fitness center, tennis, pickleball, and bocce. A larger clubhouse with pools and a restaurant is described as still being built. Current starting prices are listed on the official page and can change.
Age restriction
GL Homes labels the community Valencia Sky (55+) on its official community page and on its list of communities now selling in Naples.

Official site: GL Homes, Valencia Sky · GL Homes, About Us (Naples list).

Valencia Trails

Location
Northern Collier County (ZIP 34120). An official community newsletter lists 11880 Majestic Trails Blvd., Naples, FL 34120.
Home types
Single-family homes built by GL Homes. GL Homes’ current Naples list of communities now selling names Valencia Sky, not Valencia Trails, so buyers should plan on resale homes and confirm that with the association or a current listing.
Amenities
An official 2020 GL Homes article describes walking trails, a clubhouse, a pool, and tennis. A later official newsletter still posted on the GL Homes site also lists a clubhouse, a restaurant and bar, fitness, a racquet club and bocce, and a spa.
Age restriction
GL Homes introduced Valencia Trails as a 55+ community, and later official lifestyle materials on the GL Homes site continue to treat it as part of the Valencia 55+ program.
Good to know
The same official newsletter lists the association website at valenciatrailshoa.org.

Official site: GL Homes, introducing Valencia Trails · Official Valencia Trails newsletter (PDF).

An established 55+ neighborhood

Riviera Golf Estates

Location
East Naples. The association office is at 425 Charlemagne Blvd., Naples, FL 34112.
Home types
Single-family dwellings. The recorded declaration defines those homes as block or manufactured homes.
Amenities
The official association website lists pickleball and tennis courts, a fitness center, a community pool with a chickee hut, card and game rooms, and a clubhouse used for dinners, dances, and meetings.
Age restriction
The association’s website calls it a 55+ community. The 2025 amended declaration says the community provides housing for older persons under the Housing for Older Persons Act of 1995. Except for the federal 80/20 allowance, at least one occupant of each home must be 55 or older. Surviving spouses, and in some cases a surviving companion or an adult heir, may occupy under written exceptions, as long as the 80/20 rule is still met. The association may ask for proof of age and may conduct a census every 24 months.

Official site: Riviera Golf Estates association · 2025 amended declaration (PDF).

Cooperative and manufactured-home communities

These are also official 55+ or over-55 communities, but the purchase is often a cooperative share or a manufactured home on a lot, not a typical single-family house in a large builder neighborhood. The legal papers are different, so a buyer should read them carefully.

Enchanting Shores Cooperative

Location
17 Turquoise Ave., Naples, FL 34114, along Henderson Creek, within the Rookery Bay area.
Home types
A member-owned cooperative. Buyers should read the cooperative documents, which are different from a typical homeowners association.
Amenities
The official site lists shuffleboard, bocce, horseshoes, line dancing, pickleball, tennis, and fitness classes, plus seasonal social events. Many homes along the creek have docks, and the community has a private boat ramp.
Age restriction
The official site describes Enchanting Shores as an active-adult, over-55, pet-free co-op.
Good to know
The official FAQ dated January 26, 2026, lists quarterly assessments of $500, due January, April, July, and October 1. A unit may be leased only once per calendar year, for a minimum of 30 days and a maximum of seven months. Pets are not allowed. The same FAQ says those statements are only a summary, and a buyer should read the cooperative documents.

Official site: Enchanting Shores official site · Official FAQ, January 26, 2026 (PDF).

Riverbend of Naples

Location
North Naples, along the Cocohatchee River. Official mailing address: 777 Walkerbilt Rd. #42, Naples, FL 34110.
Home types
A member-owned cooperative of 40 homes.
Amenities
The official site lists boat slips, boat and kayak storage, and fishing docks, with access toward the Gulf at Wiggins Pass.
Age restriction
The official site describes Riverbend as an adult, over-55, member-owned cooperative.

Official site: Riverbend of Naples official site.

Hitching Post Co-Op

Location
32 Cheyenne Trail, Naples, FL 34113.
Home types
A resident-owned mobile home cooperative under Florida Statutes chapters 719 and 723. The official About page says a person may become a shareholder, who buys a share in the park, or a homeowner who rents a lot.
Amenities
The official page lists bingo, cards, shuffleboard, horseshoes, exercise classes, swimming, crafts, and social events. Monthly fees, when they apply, are described as covering general park maintenance, trash, recycling, water and sewer, and use of the recreation facilities.
Age restriction
The official About page calls it a senior park and says one resident of each home must be 55, and any other resident must be at least 40. It also says the park is governed by HUD regulations.
Good to know
The official About page publishes current monthly fees for shareholders and for lot renters. Those amounts can change, so a buyer should confirm the current figures and the difference between a share and a lot rental with the office.

Official site: Hitching Post Co-Op, About Us.

Communities not on this list

Heritage Bay in North Naples is often mentioned in 55+ searches. The club’s official rules provide for children or grandchildren age 21 or younger in a member household and include junior golf rules, so this guide does not treat it as age-restricted. Valencia Golf & Country Club shares a name with GL Homes’ Valencia 55+ communities, but it is a separate, all-ages community. Ave Maria as a whole is also for all ages; only Del Webb Naples is age-restricted there. Licensed assisted living, memory care, and continuing-care campuses are listed in the senior living directory, not here.

What to ask before buying

  1. 01

    Read the age rule in the recorded documents

    Ask for the declaration, bylaws, and any age addendum. Confirm who must be 55, what happens if a surviving spouse or adult heir is younger, and how the community checks the 80 percent occupancy rule. Source: 24 CFR 100.305–100.307.

  2. 02

    Get the current fees in writing

    Request the budget, reserve schedule, and fee sheet, including any club, cable, lawn, or gate charges. For an HOA, Florida’s official disclosure rules are in s. 720.401, Florida Statutes. Condominiums use s. 718.503. Cooperatives are covered by Chapter 719.

  3. 03

    Check everyday rules

    Leasing minimums, pets, parking, golf carts, and guest stays often matter as much as the amenities list. Enchanting Shores, for example, is pet-free and limits leases to once a year.

  4. 04

    Look up flood, insurance, and inspections

    A 55+ purchase still follows ordinary Florida buying steps: inspections, flood-zone lookup, and official closing disclosures. See Buying in Naples and Flood Zones & Insurance.

  5. 05

    Tour more than once, and ask about resale versus new construction

    Some communities on this page still sell new homes; others are resale only. Confirm that on the official builder or association site, then walk the property at more than one time of day if possible.

How this connects to future care

Buying in a 55+ community is usually a decision about a house, neighbors, and a lifestyle. It is not, by itself, a plan for long-term care. The National Institute on Aging describes long-term care as help with everyday activities such as bathing, dressing, or managing a household. That help can be provided at home, in the community, or in a facility.

The Institute also has a guide to aging in place, including home safety changes and support services, and a checklist for getting affairs in order. Those pages are general information, not medical or legal advice.

If a person later needs assisted living, memory care, or more help at home, that is a separate decision from the 55+ purchase. This site’s Senior Care guides, Independent Living page, and Helping a Parent Move guide are starting points. FAQQuestions people often ask

What does 55+ mean under federal law?

Under the Fair Housing Act’s housing-for-older-persons rules, a community can qualify as 55-or-older housing if it is intended and operated for people 55 and older, at least 80 percent of its occupied homes have at least one resident who is 55 or older, and it publishes and follows policies that show that intent. HUD’s rules are in 42 U.S.C. 3607 and 24 CFR 100.305. That 80 percent rule means a 55+ community is not always limited to residents who are all 55. Some communities, such as Riviera Golf Estates or Hitching Post, write stricter local rules on top of the federal minimum. A buyer should read the recorded documents for the community under consideration.

Are Del Webb Naples and Del Webb Ave Maria two different places?

No. Del Webb Naples official page is a 55+ community located inside Ave Maria. The Ave Maria FAQ says Ave Maria itself is for all ages, and that Del Webb Naples is the only age-restricted neighborhood there, for qualifying residents 55 and older.

Is Heritage Bay in Naples a 55+ community?

Not according to the club’s official documents reviewed for this guide. The Heritage Bay rules provide for custodial children or grandchildren age 21 or younger to use common areas when they live with a member, and they include junior golf rules. This page only lists communities whose official community, builder, or association materials say they are age-restricted or 55+.

Is a 55+ community the same as assisted living or independent living?

Usually no. The communities on this page are places where people buy a home, a villa, or a cooperative share, and live independently. They are not the same as a licensed assisted living or memory care community, or a continuing-care campus, where care is part of the contract. The National Institute on Aging, What Is Long-Term Care? explains that long-term care is help with everyday activities and can be provided at home, in the community, or in a facility. If care may be needed now or later, start with this site’s Senior Care guides. Jeff can help a family think through both the house and future care. For placement work, Jeff is paid by the communities.

How much are HOA or co-op fees?

Fees vary by community and can change. This page does not invent a typical Naples 55+ fee. Buyers should ask the association or builder for the current budget, reserve schedule, and fee sheet, and should read the official Florida disclosure package. One official example: Enchanting Shores’ Enchanting Shores official FAQ, dated January 26, 2026, lists quarterly assessments of $500. Hitching Post publishes current monthly amounts on its Hitching Post About page. Both should be confirmed with the office before a contract is signed. See also this site’s HOA & condo rules guide.

What should someone ask before buying in a 55+ community?

Ask for the recorded declaration and confirm the age rule, any exceptions for a surviving spouse or an adult heir, and how the community checks the 80 percent occupancy rule. Request the current fee schedule and budget. Review leasing, pet, vehicle, and guest rules. Look up the flood zone and insurance needs. Ask whether new homes are still for sale or only resales are available. Tour more than once if possible. A 55+ purchase is still a Florida real estate transaction, so the usual inspection, disclosure, and closing steps in the Buying in Naples guide still apply.

Can a spouse or adult child under 55 live in a 55+ community?

It depends on both federal rules and the community’s own documents. Federally, up to 20 percent of occupied homes may be occupied without a resident who is 55, unless the community writes a tighter rule. Some associations allow a surviving spouse, or in limited cases a surviving companion or an adult heir, as Riviera Golf Estates does in its Riviera Golf Estates declaration. Other communities set a minimum age for every additional resident, as Hitching Post does on its official About page. This is one of the first things to confirm in writing.
