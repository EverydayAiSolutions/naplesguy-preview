A break for the family caregiver

Respite Care in Naples, FL

Respite care in Naples, FL gives a family caregiver a planned break, for a few hours, a day or a short stay, while trained staff care for the older adult. Options include in-home help, adult day programs, dementia respite groups and short stays in assisted living.

Type 1

In-home respite care in Naples

A home care agency sends a companion or aide for a few hours or overnight. CareScout's 2025 Florida median for a non-medical home caregiver is $32 an hour, and many agencies set a minimum number of hours per visit.

Check any agency's license or registration on FloridaHealthFinder before the first visit, and ask who supervises the caregiver.

Type 2

Day programs and dementia respite groups

Licensed adult day care centers in Naples include respite among their basic services under Florida law. CareScout's 2025 Florida median is $75 a day.

For people living with memory loss, Baker Senior Center Naples runs a Dementia Respite Program on weekdays from 10:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Participants can attend up to three four-hour groups a week, led by credentialed social workers and music therapists, with a hot lunch (239-325-4444).

Type 3

Short respite stays in assisted living

Many assisted living and memory care communities in Naples accept short stays when an apartment is open, often with a minimum length of stay. Florida still requires the admission steps, including a medical examination within 60 days before or 30 days after admission (section 429.26).

A short stay is also a gentle way to try a community before a permanent move. Good to know

Questions people ask

What is respite care?

Respite care is short-term care for an older adult so the usual caregiver can rest, travel, work or handle appointments. Florida law lists respite as one of the basic services of a licensed adult day care center.

Is there dementia respite in Naples?

Yes. Baker Senior Center Naples runs a Dementia Respite Program Monday through Friday, 10:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.; participants can attend up to three four-hour groups a week, led by credentialed social workers and music therapists. The center notes that some of its services carry fees; call 239-325-4444.

Does the VA pay for respite care?

VA respite care can pay for a home health aide, an adult day health care program or a short nursing home stay. Nursing home respite is limited to 30 days per calendar year, and a copay may apply. The Veteran must be enrolled in VA health care and meet clinical criteria.

Can a parent stay in assisted living for a short respite stay?

Many Naples assisted living communities offer short stays when they have an open apartment. Florida still requires the admission paperwork, including a medical examination, and each community decides whether it can meet the person's needs.

Who can help find respite care in Collier County?

Call the Elder Helpline at 866-413-5337 (Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida), the Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline at 800-272-3900, or Jeff Silberman at 609-575-8626 for short-stay options.
