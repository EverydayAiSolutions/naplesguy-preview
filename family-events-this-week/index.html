Naples Events This Week: common questions

What events are in Naples this week?
This page covers family events dated November 18–22, 2026 (today through Sunday, Eastern Time). Mid-week it includes remaining weekdays plus the coming weekend.

How is this different from the weekend list?
The weekend page is only Saturday and Sunday. This week page also includes leftover weekdays from today through Friday, when those days are still ahead.

Where do the listings come from?
Listings are taken from official organizer pages and public calendars, including Collier County Parks & Recreation Events Directory, Visit Naples (Paradise Coast) events, Collier County Public Library events.
