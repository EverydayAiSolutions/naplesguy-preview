Senior placement in Marco Island

Marco Island is part of Collier County. There are fewer assisted living facilities on the island than in mainland Naples, so many families also look at East Naples and the rest of Collier County.

Counts include licensed facilities whose FloridaHealthFinder city is Marco Island or whose address uses ZIP 34145.

FAQ

Senior placement questions for Marco Island

How many licensed assisted living facilities are in Marco Island?

The directory lists 1 assisted living facilities and 0 nursing homes or rehab facilities for Marco Island. Licenses can change, so look up any community on FloridaHealthFinder before you decide.

Which hospitals are near Marco Island?

The official NCH and Physicians Regional location directories used for this page do not list a hospital campus on Marco Island. The closest campuses listed are Physicians Regional Medical Center - Collier Boulevard in East Naples and NCH Baker Hospital in the City of Naples. Confirm current emergency and transfer arrangements with the hospital and with local emergency services. Official campus addresses and links are listed on this page. A hospital discharge after an inpatient stay is often when families start looking at skilled nursing or rehab; Medicare rules for that stay are explained on the skilled nursing guide.

Do any communities in Marco Island accept Medicaid?

0 assisted living facilities and 0 nursing homes in this Marco Island group have Medicaid listed as a payment form on the official FloridaHealthFinder profile or, for nursing homes, as Medicaid participation on Medicare Care Compare. A payment-form listing is not a promise that a Medicaid bed is open. Confirm directly with the facility and, if enrolled, with the Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care plan. The Medicaid facilities page lists matching communities in Collier County and nearby Lee County (Bonita Springs, Estero, and Fort Myers).
