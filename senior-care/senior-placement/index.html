What care costs in Florida

The CareScout Cost of Care Survey 2025, document 282102 (dated 03/02/26), from a survey collected July through November 2025, reports a Florida median of $5,610 a month for assisted living. See the Naples senior living cost guide and the official CareScout Cost of Care tool.

Senior placement questions families often ask

What is senior placement in Naples?

He has 15 years of experience in geriatric and senior care, and he helps families with every step of the search and the move. He also helps with assisted living placement and memory care placement, and with questions about the house, as a licensed real estate agent.

Do families pay Jeff for senior placement services?

Jeff is paid by the communities, so families don’t have to worry about it. He guides families through every step of finding and moving into senior living, and can help with the house as a licensed real estate agent.

How do Jeff’s senior placement services work?

Jeff goes over options with the family one-on-one. He helps them understand which type of care may fit, using official sources and the Which Care Fits guide. Families can review state-licensed communities in the Senior Living Directory and look up a license on FloridaHealthFinder. Jeff helps with every step of the search and the move. If a house needs to be sold or bought as part of that move, he can help as a licensed real estate agent. For real estate, Jeff will figure everything out with you.

Which areas does Jeff serve?

Jeff works with families in Naples first, then elsewhere in Collier County, and in nearby Bonita Springs, Estero, and Fort Myers. The Senior Living Directory lists currently licensed communities in those same areas.

Can Jeff help with assisted living or memory care placement?

Yes. Jeff helps families looking at assisted living placement, memory care placement, home care, and skilled nursing or rehab in Naples and nearby. Start with a call, or read the Assisted Living and Memory Care guides.

How can a family check that a community is licensed?

Use FloridaHealthFinder, the official Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) facility locator, to look up a Florida license, inspection information, and related reports.

Where else can families get official help in Collier County?

The statewide Florida Elder Helpline is 1-800-963-5337 (the official page also lists 239-652-6901 for Collier County). The Area Agency on Aging for Southwest Florida, the Aging and Disability Resource Center that serves Collier County, is listed at 239-652-6900 or toll-free 866-413-5337. Those official numbers, with source links, are also on the Local Help page.
