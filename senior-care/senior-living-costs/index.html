How to use these figures

A median is a benchmark, not a Naples quote

Florida AHCA says each assisted living provider sets its own fees and may charge a basic fee plus extras. The resident contract has to spell out the services included and the rates. Florida law generally requires at least 30 days written notice of a rate increase, with a limited exception for a new service not previously charged.

Use the figures below to understand the range, then ask each Naples-area community for a current written rate, what is included, and how the rate can change. If a community has not published a current price, this guide treats it as call for pricing. See also the Costs & Paying guide and the cost worksheet.

Sources: Florida AHCA, assisted living guide; Florida Statutes s. 429.24.

CareScout 2025

Official Cost of Care figures

The CareScout Cost of Care Survey 2025 collected provider-reported rates from July through November 2025. What changes the bill

What affects price in Naples

  • Level of care. AHCA notes that a community may charge a basic fee plus extras. A higher need for help with bathing, dressing, medication, or meals can raise the monthly amount.
  • Memory care add-ons. A secured memory-care neighborhood or added dementia staffing is often priced separately. Ask for the memory-care rate in writing, and confirm whether it is in addition to the assisted living fee. If that number is not published, call for pricing.
  • Season. Southwest Florida has a busy winter season. Ask whether available rooms, waitlists, or rates change between the summer and the winter season. This guide does not invent a seasonal surcharge; it is a question to ask.
  • Move-in fees and rate increases. Ask about any community fee or deposit, how refunds work, and how much notice is given before a rate change. Florida law generally requires at least 30 days written notice of a rate increase.

Sources: Florida AHCA, assisted living guide; Florida Statutes s. 429.24.

Medicare, Medicaid, and VA

Official programs that may help — and their dates

Medicare does not pay the monthly assisted living fee. For Original Medicare skilled nursing facility care in 2026, Medicare.gov lists $0 per day for days 1-20 after the Part A deductible, $217 per day for days 21-100, and all costs after day 100 in a benefit period. Those 2026 figures are from Medicare.gov and can change in later years.

Florida Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care program can cover assisted living or nursing home care for people who qualify. An official AHCA FAQ labeled for 2026 states that non-working individuals in included waivers, including the Long-Term Care Waiver, have a monthly income limit of $2,982 for an individual or $5,964 for a couple, and an asset limit of $2,000 for an individual or $3,000 for a couple. Those figures can change, and the Department of Children and Families decides financial eligibility. There can be a waitlist after an ADRC screening. See Does Medicaid pay for assisted living in Florida? and the list of facilities that list Medicaid.

Qualified Veterans or surviving spouses who already receive a VA pension may apply for VA Aid and Attendance or Housebound benefits. This page does not reprint a 2026 dollar amount unless it is confirmed on the official VA page at the time of writing; use the VA page for current payment rates.

Sources: Medicare.gov, 2026 costs; Florida AHCA, SMMC Long-Term Care; AHCA, Working People with Disabilities FAQ 2026; VA Aid and Attendance and Housebound allowance.

FAQ

Cost questions families often ask

What does assisted living cost in Naples?

The CareScout Cost of Care Survey 2025, collected from July through November 2025, lists a Florida median of $5,610 a month, or $67,320 a year, for an assisted living community. CareScout also publishes metro-level medians in its official tool, including for the Naples and Cape Coral-Fort Myers areas, but the official PDF tables fetched for this page are statewide and national. Individual Naples communities set their own fees. If a current written rate has not been published, treat the figure as call for pricing.

Why not use only a national or Florida average?

A statewide median is a benchmark, not a Naples quote. Florida AHCA says each provider sets its own fees and may charge a basic fee plus extras. Naples and Collier County prices can be higher or lower than the Florida median. Ask each community for a current written rate, what is included, and how the rate can change. Florida law generally requires at least 30 days written notice of a rate increase, with a limited exception for a new service not previously charged.

What usually affects the price in Naples?

The monthly amount often depends on the level of care a person needs, the size or type of apartment, and add-on services. Memory care, medication management, incontinence care, and escort help to meals or activities may be extra. Seasonal demand in Southwest Florida can also affect availability; ask whether rates or available rooms change in the winter season. Unpublished prices should be marked call for pricing.

Does Medicaid or the VA help pay?

Medicare does not pay the monthly assisted living fee. Florida Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care program can cover assisted living or nursing home care for people who qualify medically and financially; there can be a waitlist. An official AHCA FAQ labeled for 2026 lists a monthly income limit of $2,982 for a non-working individual in included waivers, including the Long-Term Care Waiver, or $5,964 for a couple, and asset limits of $2,000 or $3,000. Those figures can change, and the Department of Children and Families decides financial eligibility. Qualified Veterans or survivors who already receive a VA pension may apply for VA Aid and Attendance or Housebound benefits. Confirm each path on the official AHCA, DCF, or VA page.

How does home care compare with a move?

CareScout 2025 Florida median for a non-medical caregiver is $32 an hour, or $6,101 a month at the survey 44-hours-a-week basis ($73,216 a year). That is not a 24-hour quote. Medicare home health is separate and does not pay for 24-hour custodial care at home. The home care versus moving page explains how to compare those official figures with a community written rate.
