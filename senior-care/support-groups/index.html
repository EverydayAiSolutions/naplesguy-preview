Alzheimer’s Support Network in Naples: Support Groups, Programs & Calendar

Our organization exists to enrich the lives of people living with Alzheimer’s and other forms of neurodegeneration and ease the lives of their care partners (caregivers).

— Alzheimer’s Support Network, support.network

The Alzheimer’s Support Network says its services are always free of charge, and that sponsors and private donors have allowed it to provide all of its services free of charge for more than 40 years.

Programs

What the Network offers

Each program below is described from the Alzheimer’s Support Network’s official pages, with a link to the exact page. The full list is also on their programs page.

Care Guiding and private family meetings

Family consultants (care guides) meet with families in person, by phone, Zoom, or email. The Network likes to schedule a private meeting with new families to learn about the situation. It does not perform cognitive assessments, memory screenings, or formal interviews, and it follows a social rather than medical model. In-person meetings take place in Collier County; families elsewhere can meet by Zoom. To make an appointment, call 239-262-8388 or email help@support.network.

Open the official page

Support groups

The programs page says the Network has over 30 support groups each month, including disease-specific groups such as Lewy body, FTD, or vascular groups, groups for special audiences such as adult children or 12 Step groups, and general support groups for anyone who wishes to come. The current month’s times are in the schedule on this page and on ASN’s own calendar.

Open the official page

Brain Fitness Center and Club Room

During general support groups, the Network hosts its Brain Fitness Center / Club Room for people living with Alzheimer’s and other forms of brain change, through brain stimulation, socialization, and chair yoga. All are welcome no matter the type or stage of disease. Care partners must remain in the building.

Open the official page

Kindred Spirits

Kindred Spirits is a discussion group for people in the early stages of cognitive impairment who want to discuss their concerns and challenges openly in a group format.

Open the official page

Ladies & Gentlemen’s Clubs

Held at Terracina Grand on Fridays, these clubs are designed to provide engaging activities for people living with brain change and a break for care partners. The official engagement page says there is no cost to attend, but registration is required, and the clubs have been running for more than 15 years. The November 2026 calendar lists them on Fridays, 12:30–3:30 p.m., and asks people to email Debbie@Support.Network about eligibility. There is no club on Friday, November 27, 2026.

Open the official page

Wanderer’s ID Program

Offered with the Collier County Sheriff’s Office, this program collects vital information and a current photograph and provides a medical alert bracelet or necklace so a person who wanders can be identified and returned home. An application and a caregiver release are required. The official program page says there is no charge.

Open the official page

Education meetings and recorded education

The Network brings in experts for educational events on dementia, caregiving, and related topics. Some meetings are recorded so families can watch later. Official recorded lectures include monthly talks by Dr. William Justiz and Teepa Snow, collected on the educational videos and monthly lecture pages.

Special events and community adventures

The calendar includes special speakers and other events. The engagement page also lists community adventures designed for people living with cognitive changes, including Botanical Garden sensory tours, Nature’s Peace guided tours, and Shy Wolf Sanctuary, plus Meet Me in the Garden with the Naples Botanical Garden.

Naples Unites and volunteer opportunities

Naples Unites is described as a way for local businesses to offer volunteer opportunities so people with Alzheimer’s can take part in meaningful activities, and to raise awareness and lessen stigma. The Network also invites people to contact the office about volunteering in its own programs.

Open the official page

Professional education, including the Virtual Dementia Tour

The Network holds educational lectures, seminars, and workshops for families and for professionals, including programs designed for people who work with those living with Alzheimer’s. It says two staff members are certified trainers of the Virtual Dementia Tour, an experiential tool that can be offered in the office or off-site. The November 2026 education calendar lists Virtual Dementia Tour appointments on Wednesday, November 11, in Suite 230, in person only.

Questions families ask

Common questions

Are Alzheimer’s Support Network programs free?

The Alzheimer’s Support Network says its services are always free of charge, and that sponsors and private donors have allowed it to provide all of its services free of charge for more than 40 years. Some activities, such as the Ladies & Gentlemen’s Clubs, still require registration.

How do families reach the Alzheimer’s Support Network in Naples?

The official contact details are 1421 Pine Ridge Rd, Suite 100, Naples, FL 34109; phone 239-262-8388; email info@support.network or help@support.network. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Appointments with family consultants can be made by phone or by emailing help@support.network.

Are support groups in person or online?

Many groups are hybrid (in person or online). Some are online only, such as Adult Children. The current month’s calendar is the best guide, especially around holidays.

What is the Wanderer’s ID Program?

It is a program offered by the Alzheimer’s Support Network with the Collier County Sheriff’s Office. Families complete an application, provide a current photograph, use an identification bracelet or necklace, and sign a caregiver release so a person who wanders can be identified and returned home. The official program page says there is no charge.

Is this page an official Alzheimer’s Support Network website?

No. This page is a local guide on The Naples Guy that describes the Network’s published programs and the current official calendar, with links to the exact pages on support.network. It is not a partnership or an endorsement. For the most current times, use the Network’s own calendar or call 239-262-8388.
