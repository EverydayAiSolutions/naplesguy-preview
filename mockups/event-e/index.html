I. The occasion

Handmade gifts, Santa, and hot chocolate, all under one roof.

The Artisan Market Christmas Event fills The Artisan Market of Naples with holiday cheer on Friday, December 11, from 3:00 to 8:00 PM, in Saint Andrews Plaza on Tamiami Trail East.

Local artists and makers bring one-of-a-kind gifts, so it is an easy place to check off the Christmas list. Kids can meet Santa, and everyone can warm up with hot chocolate and cookies.

  • Christmas shopping

    Handmade gifts from local artists and makers.

  • Meet Santa

    Bring the kids for a festive holiday visit.

  • Hot chocolate & cookies

    A cozy treat while you shop.
