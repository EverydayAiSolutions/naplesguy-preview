What to expect

Good to know before you go

Big Top Christmas, the 4th Annual Student Holiday Showcase at Citrus Circus Center, is Saturday, December 5, at 24850 Old 41 Rd in Bonita Springs. Doors open at 5:00 PM and the show begins at 5:30 PM.

The family-friendly program includes silks, trapeze, lyra, hammock, roller skate, and a dog act, with light refreshments. Tickets are $25 on the official ticket page.

Details can change. Please confirm on the official page before heading out.
