What to expect

Big Top Christmas, the 4th Annual Student Holiday Showcase at Citrus Circus Center, is Saturday, Dec 5, 2026, at 24850 Old 41 Rd, Suite 23, Bonita Springs.

Doors open at 5:00 PM and the show begins at 5:30 PM. The family-friendly program includes silks, trapeze, lyra, hammock, roller skate, and a dog act, with light refreshments.

Tickets are $25 on the official ticket page. Official information is on the Citrus Circus Center website.

Details come from the official listing and can change. Please confirm on the official event page before heading out.
