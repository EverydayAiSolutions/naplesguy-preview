What to expect

Skip the mall. Shop the makers.

Black Friday Weekend at Artisan Market Naples runs Friday, November 27 through Sunday, November 29, at The Artisan Market of Naples in Saint Andrews Plaza.

Shoppers will find sales and specials from local artists, along with handmade items and handcrafted foods. The flyer does not list weekend hours, so please check the official page before going.
