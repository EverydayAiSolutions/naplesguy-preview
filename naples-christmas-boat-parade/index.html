Naples Christmas Boat Parade 2026

The Naples Christmas Boat Parade 2026 glides across Naples Bay on Saturday, December 19, from 6:15 to 8:30 PM, with decorated boats hosted by the Marine Industries Association of Collier County (MIACC).

On the water

About the Christmas Boat Parade on Naples Bay

Decorated boats parade across Naples Bay each December. The Marine Industries Association of Collier County (MIACC) hosts the parade, the judges and VIP guests at the Naples City Dock, and 100% of the proceeds benefit Ricky King and the MIACC Foundation.

Boaters who want to join can use the entry form on the MIACC page. The MIACC office answers questions at 239-682-0900.

Good to know

Questions people ask

When is the Naples Christmas Boat Parade 2026?

Saturday, December 19, 2026, from 6:15 to 8:30 PM on Naples Bay, according to the Marine Industries Association of Collier County.

Where is the best place to watch the Naples boat parade?

MIACC lists these viewing spots: Naples City Dock (ticketed), Pinchers Tin City, Riverwalk Restaurant, Naples Landing, Kelly's Fish House, The Boat House on Naples Bay and Bayview Park.

Do I need a ticket to watch the boat parade?

Tickets are sold for the Naples City Dock, where MIACC hosts the parade, the judges and VIP guests. MIACC also lists public parks such as Naples Landing and Bayview Park among the viewing spots.

Can I enter my boat in the Christmas boat parade?

Boaters can apply with the entry form on the MIACC boat parade page, or call the MIACC office at 239-682-0900.

Who does the boat parade benefit?

MIACC says 100% of the proceeds benefit Ricky King and the MIACC Foundation.
