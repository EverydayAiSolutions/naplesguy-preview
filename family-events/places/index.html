What kids can do

Audubon sanctuary with immersive exhibits in the Spurlino Foundation Discovery Center / Blair Visitor Center and a 2.25-mile boardwalk through old-growth bald cypress forest. A children’s scavenger hunt activity sheet is available at the admissions desk.

Ages
Children under 6 are free. Child admission is listed for ages 6 to 14.
Price / admission
General admission $17; full-time college student with valid school ID $10; children 6 to 14 $6; children under 6 free; Corkscrew members free; U.S. military with ID $14; National Audubon members with ID $14. No same-day refunds.
Hours
See hours on the official website
Rules
May 1–Dec. 15: open 8:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. (enter until 1 p.m.; boardwalk closes at 3 p.m.). Dec. 16–April 30: open 8:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. (enter until 3 p.m.; boardwalk closes at 5 p.m.). Open on holidays. Obtaining tickets online is recommended. No pets (ADA service animals only). No drones. Parts of the boardwalk may be slippery when wet.
