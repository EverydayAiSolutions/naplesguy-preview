Gulfshore Playhouse presents A Christmas Carol, adapted and directed by Kristen Coury, on the Moran Mainstage at the Baker Theatre and Education Center, 100 Goodlette-Frank Road South, Naples. Previews are Nov 17, 18, and 19; opening night is Friday, Nov 20 at 7:30 PM; Saturday, Nov 21 has performances at 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; the final performance is Dec 24. The production features live music, dancing, and Dickens’ story of Scrooge’s journey through Christmases past, present, and future. Tickets are sold through Gulfshore Playhouse.

Questions families ask

Rainy Day Activities in Naples for Kids: common questions

What can kids do in Naples on a rainy day?
This page highlights upcoming indoor events from the Family Events calendar for the next two weeks, such as library programs, museum days, theater performances, and gym classes. The Things to Do Anytime page also lists indoor spots you can visit without a special event.

Does this include beaches or parks?
Outdoor-only listings such as beaches and splash pads are left off this rainy-day list. If an official event is clearly indoors, it can appear here even when the venue also has outdoor grounds.

How current is the list?
The list is built from the same Family Events calendar that updates when official listings are imported. Always confirm hours on the official page, especially when storms affect opening times.
