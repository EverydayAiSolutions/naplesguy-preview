Holiday Events in Naples, FL: common questions

Where can I find holiday events in Naples?
This page gathers holidays and festivals from the Family Events calendar for 2026. Thanksgiving and Christmas also have their own pages when you want a narrower list.

Which official calendars list holiday programs?
Start with Collier County Parks & Recreation Events Directory, Visit Naples (Paradise Coast) events, Collier County Public Library events, then confirm on the organizer’s own page.

Do hours change on holidays?
Often yes. Parks, libraries, and museums may close or shorten hours on federal holidays. Check the official page for the date you plan to go.
