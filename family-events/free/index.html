The official Girls Golf Family Golf Clinic at the CME Group Tour Championship is Saturday, Nov 21, at the Tiburón Golf Club Driving Range, 1st Tee Side, in Naples. Registration opens 2:00–2:30 PM. The 5-E Stations clinic runs 2:30–4:00 PM: full swing, chipping, putting, a yoga/relay/hula-hoop station, and a craft, hydrate, and snack station. Parents must register to reserve a spot. All juniors and chaperoning adults receive free tournament admission. Parking for clinic attendees starts at 2:00 PM at no charge. Equipment is provided; juniors may bring their own. Questions families ask

Free Things to Do in Naples with Kids: common questions

What free things can families do in Naples with kids?
This page collects upcoming events whose official listing is marked free, such as many library storytimes and some parks or community programs. Always confirm on the official page, because a registration or a suggested donation can still apply.

Are all library programs free?
Many Collier County Public Library programs are free to attend, and they are listed on the library events directory. Registration can still be required. Check the official library listing for the program you want.

Do free events include nearby towns?
Yes. Naples listings come first, then free events in Estero, Bonita Springs, and Fort Myers.
