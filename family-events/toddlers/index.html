Toddler Activities in Naples, FL: common questions

Where can I find toddler activities in Naples?
This page collects upcoming programs whose official listing is aimed at toddlers, infants, or storytime. Many take place at Collier County Public Library branches and are also listed on the library events directory.

Do I need to register for storytime?
Some library and museum programs ask families to register, even when attendance is free. The official event page lists the age range, the branch, and whether registration is open.

Are these only in Naples?
Naples programs are listed first. Storytimes and toddler programs in Estero, Bonita Springs, and Fort Myers appear in the nearby group.
