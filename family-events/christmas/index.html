Grand Illuminations at The Village Shops on Venetian Bay is Saturday, Nov 21, 2026, from 3:00–7:00 PM at 4200 Gulf Shore Blvd North, Naples. Gulfshore Playhouse presents A Christmas Carol, adapted and directed by Kristen Coury, on the Moran Mainstage at the Baker Theatre and Education Center, 100 Goodlette-Frank Road South, Naples. Previews are Nov 17, 18, and 19; opening night is Friday, Nov 20 at 7:30 PM; Saturday, Nov 21 has performances at 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; the final performance is Dec 24. The production features live music, dancing, and Dickens’ story of Scrooge’s journey through Christmases past, present, and future. Tickets are sold through Gulfshore Playhouse.
